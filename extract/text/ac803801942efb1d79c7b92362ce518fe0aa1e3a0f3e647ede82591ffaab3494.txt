REMARKS
ZFW reported thatthepilot of SKW4525,DFW to SLC, said he observed a UFO that had been matching his speed direction and altitude (FL360) for the past 15 to 20 minutes. The object had alternating red, white and green lights and was reported to be approximately 40 miles west of the WINK VOR. Subsequently, near the same geographic position N449TM, BE40 observed the same thing but stated it was at his altitude (FL410) and additionally had a flashing yellow light. Neither ZFW or HQ DEN observed a target in the area. At 1256, both pilots reported to ZFW that the object had faded and was no longer observed. HQ DEN contacted CONR, NORAD and AMOC. All stated they observed nothing in the area. All DEN participants will report any additional sitings.
