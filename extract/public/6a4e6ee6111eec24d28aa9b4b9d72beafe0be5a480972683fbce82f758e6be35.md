# Seven Federal Agents Reported Multiple Unidentified Objects Over Western US

**Department of War · 2023 · Western United States**

> A Department of War briefing summarizes witness statements from seven federal law enforcement agents who observed several distinct unidentified phenomena — glowing orbs, a triangular "kite," and a semi-transparent object — over two days in 2023 in a restricted area of the western United States. The report is based entirely on witness testimony with no sensor data or technical analysis included.

This document is a summary briefing compiled by the Department of War. It collects statements from seven federal law enforcement special agents (identified only as USPER1 through USPER7) who reported observing multiple unidentified anomalous phenomena during two days in 2023, at dusk and pre-dawn hours, in a restricted zone in the western United States.

The agents described four types of objects. First, three pairs of agents independently reported seeing orange orbs in the sky that emitted or launched smaller red orbs in groups of two to four. This was reported to have occurred at least five times over the two days, with the orange orb visible for only one or two seconds each time. Second, the agents observed a large, stationary, fiery orange orb — compared by one witness to the Eye of Sauron or an orange bowling ball — near a rock formation. It made no noticeable sound and was visible for about one minute. The witnesses estimated it was 500–600 meters away, but the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) later measured the distance at roughly 1,050 meters and assessed the object's size at 12–18 meters in diameter — significantly larger than the agents initially believed.

Two other objects were reported the same night. One, called the "Dark Kite," was initially mistaken for a car because it displayed a red and a white light about 2–3 feet off the ground and moved along a road at an estimated 15–20 mph. When agents pursued it, the object suddenly moved laterally off the road over desert terrain without changing its orientation relative to the observers, stopping about 100 meters off the road before turning off its lights. It was later described to AARO as triangular and about 4 feet wide. Roughly 30 minutes later, a second kite-shaped object — dubbed the "Transparent Kite" — was seen about 6 meters off the ground. Witnesses reported they could faintly see stars through it, suggesting partial transparency. One of the three agents present during this sighting did not see the object at all. During this encounter, an agent shining a spotlight reported the beam appeared to stop at about 50 yards on nothing visible.

The document does not offer any technical explanation or analysis of conventional causes for any of the sightings. No radar data, photographs, or sensor readings accompany the report. AARO's involvement is limited to correcting distance and size estimates and receiving descriptions from the agents after the fact. The specific location within the western United States is not identified beyond being described as a restricted zone.

## What to know

- All observations come from witness testimony alone — no radar tracks, photographs, video, or sensor data are included in or referenced by this report.
- Witness distance estimates were significantly off: agents estimated 500–600 meters for the large orb, but AARO measurements put it at roughly 1,050 meters, nearly double. This suggests visual estimates in these conditions were unreliable.
- One of three agents present during the Transparent Kite sighting did not see the object, raising questions about visibility and observing conditions.
- The document is a summary of witness statements, not the original reports themselves, and it provides no technical analysis or discussion of possible conventional explanations.
- The sightings occurred during low-light conditions (dusk and pre-dawn) in an unspecified restricted zone, limiting both visual reliability and the ability to independently verify the events.
