# Two Aircraft Spotted Balloon-Shaped Object at 43,000 Feet in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA SKYWATCH report documents a February 2023 sighting in which a commercial aircraft (callsign PRD57) at 43,000 feet observed a balloon-shaped object moving eastbound, roughly 30 miles east of GRD. A second aircraft independently confirmed the same object. The case was closed with no identification or explanation recorded.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report — a standardised form the Federal Aviation Administration uses to log unidentified aerial phenomenon sightings reported by pilots. It records an event from 17 February 2023 involving a commercial aircraft operated by PRD, flying under callsign PRD57.

The crew reported spotting a balloon-shaped object while flying southbound at 43,000 feet, approximately 30 miles east of GRD. The object was moving eastbound. A second aircraft, registered as N850VP (a Hawker 800-series business jet), was flying southbound at 40,000 feet and independently reported seeing the same object. The Air Watch Office (AWO) was notified.

The report is marked as Closed. No resolution, identification, or follow-up findings are included in the document. There is no radar data, imagery, or sensor evidence attached — only the two pilot accounts.

The timing is notable: February 2023 was the period immediately following the Chinese surveillance balloon incidents over the United States, when airspace awareness was heightened and pilots were reporting balloon sightings at an elevated rate. The document does not connect this sighting to those events, nor does it rule out any explanation.

## What to know

- This report contains only pilot eyewitness accounts — no radar tracks, photographs, or sensor data are included.
- The object is described solely as 'balloon-shaped' with no detail on size, altitude, colour, or behaviour beyond eastbound movement.
- The case is marked Closed with no identification or explanation provided, which means the FAA neither confirmed nor ruled out any cause.
- The sighting occurred in February 2023, during a period of heightened balloon-related reports following the Chinese surveillance balloon incidents — context that may have influenced reporting patterns.
- Two independent aircraft witnessed the object, which adds some corroboration, but both observations appear to come from the same brief window with no additional follow-up documented.
