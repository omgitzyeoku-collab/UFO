# Airline Pilot Reported Unidentified Object Above 37,000 Feet Over Arkansas

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA notification records that the crew of American Airlines flight 2561, a Boeing 737-800 flying from Dallas/Fort Worth to Charlotte, reported a possible UFO above their cruising altitude of 37,000 feet over Little Rock, Arkansas on 7 August 2022. The document is a brief operational alert relayed by air traffic control, not a full investigation.

This document is an incident notification from the FAA's Washington Operations Center — essentially an internal alert that something unusual was reported during a commercial flight. It is not an investigation report and contains no conclusions about what the object was.

On 7 August 2022, at approximately 1:30 AM Central time, the crew of American Airlines flight 2561 reported a possible UFO sighting while cruising at flight level 370 (roughly 37,000 feet) over Little Rock, Arkansas. The object was described only as being above the aircraft's altitude. Memphis Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC), the regional air traffic control facility handling the flight, relayed the report to FAA Operations.

The flight was a Boeing 737-800 operating the Dallas/Fort Worth to Charlotte route. The coordinates recorded in the report (34.75°N, 92.29°W) correspond to the Little Rock area.

No description of the object's appearance, behaviour, speed, or duration of the sighting is included in the document. The pilot himself characterised it as a "possible" UFO sighting, indicating uncertainty. The document is categorised under "Miscellaneous" — a general FAA reporting bucket rather than a specific threat or safety category.

There is no indication of any follow-up investigation, radar data, or corroborating reports from other aircraft or ground observers in this document.
