# Sun Country Pilot Reported High-Altitude Balloon Over Kansas in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a Sun Country Airlines pilot observing a large balloon at roughly 50,000 feet while flying at 36,000 feet over Kansas on 3 February 2023. The report was filed through the FAA's SKYWATCH system and is now closed with no follow-up investigation details included.

On 3 February 2023, the crew of a Sun Country Airlines Boeing 737-800 (callsign SCX623) spotted a large balloon while flying southbound from Minneapolis to Harlingen, Texas. The aircraft was cruising at 36,000 feet when the crew observed the object off the right side of the plane, roughly 25 nautical miles southeast of Pratt, Kansas.

The crew estimated the balloon was at approximately 50,000 feet or higher — well above their own altitude and above the ceiling of most commercial aircraft. The sighting was reported through the FAA's Denver office and handled by Kansas City Center, the air traffic control facility covering that region.

The report was logged in the FAA's SKYWATCH system, which tracks unidentified aerial phenomena reported by pilots. The case status is listed as closed. The single-page document contains no details of any follow-up investigation, no radar or sensor data, and no determination of what the balloon was or where it came from.

The date of this sighting — 3 February 2023 — falls during a period when several high-altitude balloons were detected over US airspace, including a widely reported Chinese surveillance balloon. This report does not draw any connection to those events, and the object's identity remains unconfirmed.
