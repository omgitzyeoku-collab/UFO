# WestJet Crew Reported Balloon-Like Object at 36,000 Feet in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a sighting by commercial flight WJA1100 of an unidentified aerial phenomenon described as a balloon on 3 February 2023. The aircraft was flying westbound at 36,000 feet when the crew reported the object to Kansas City Center. The case was closed with no further action indicated.

On 3 February 2023, the crew of WestJet flight WJA1100 — a commercial aircraft — reported spotting an unidentified aerial phenomenon while flying westbound at flight level 360 (approximately 36,000 feet). The object was described parenthetically in the report as a "Balloon," though it was formally coded under the FAA's primary category of Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon.

The sighting was reported to Kansas City Center (ZKC), an FAA air-traffic control facility covering a large section of the central United States. The object's position was logged at BQS090025, a navigational reference point. No specific incident location beyond this reference is provided in the report.

The FAA classified the report under its SKYWATCH system and subsequently closed the case. No diversion of the aircraft or further investigative action is indicated in the document.

The timing is notable: this sighting occurred in early February 2023, during a period of heightened public and governmental attention to balloon-related aerial objects following the Chinese surveillance balloon incident over the United States. Whether this context influenced the "Balloon" description is not stated in the record.
