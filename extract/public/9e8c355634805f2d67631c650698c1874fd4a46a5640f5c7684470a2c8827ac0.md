# FAA Report: Pilot Saw Unexplained Light Above Aircraft Near Bimini

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA SKYWATCH incident report documents a general aviation pilot's sighting of an unidentified bright light above their aircraft while flying at 45,000 feet near Bimini on 9 November 2020. The case was closed with no identification or resolution noted.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report filed under record number 237UAP00042. It records a UFO sighting reported by a general aviation pilot on 9 November 2020. The aircraft, flying from Houston Intercontinental Airport (IAH) to Piarco International Airport in Trinidad (TTPP), was at flight level 450 — roughly 45,000 feet — and heading southeast near the ZBV navigational point at Bimini when the pilot-in-command reported an unidentified object above them.

The pilot described the object as a light "brighter than any star they had ever seen." No evasive action was taken. Miami Center (ZMA), the FAA facility handling the aircraft, forwarded the report to the UFO Reporting Center.

The report was categorised under the primary code "OTHER" and its status is listed as closed. No resolution, identification, or follow-up findings are recorded in the document. There is no mention of corroborating radar data, sensor records, or additional witnesses.

One notable discrepancy: the incident occurred in November 2020, but the record carries a 2023 designation in its metadata, suggesting it was catalogued or released well after the event took place.
