# USAF F-35 pilot reported sensor and visual detection of unidentified object over Arizona

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA operations centre notification records a February 2023 incident in which a US Air Force F-35 fighter jet detected an unidentified object both on its sensors and visually while flying near Glendale, Arizona. The pilot could not identify the object, which was travelling in a different direction and at a higher altitude than the jet.

This document is an FAA Washington Operations Center incident notification dated 27 February 2023. It is a preliminary internal report, not a completed investigation or case file. The notification was relayed by Albuquerque Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC), the air-traffic facility responsible for that airspace.

According to the report, a US Air Force F-35 fighter jet operating under the callsign BLOOD01 was flying westbound at 12,500 feet, approximately 68 nautical miles west-northwest of Luke Air Force Base near Glendale, Arizona. At 12:10 PM local time (MST), the pilot reported both a sensor hit and a visual sighting of an object at the aircraft's 2 o'clock position. The unidentified object was travelling northeast at flight level 185 — roughly 18,500 feet, some 6,000 feet above the F-35.

The pilot in command could not identify the object. The document provides no description of the object's shape, size, speed, or behaviour beyond its heading and altitude. There is no indication that the FAA or any other agency conducted a follow-up investigation based on this notification alone.

The report shows minimal redaction. It is a single-page operational log entry rather than a detailed analytical product. No radar data, photographs, or additional witness accounts are included.
