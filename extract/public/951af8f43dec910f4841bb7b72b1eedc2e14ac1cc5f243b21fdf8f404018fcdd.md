# Citizen Reports Green Falling Object Near Merrill Iowa — Resolved as Meteor

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA Washington Operations Center report documents a citizen sighting of a green, car-sized object falling from the sky near Merrill, Iowa on 1 February 2023. The Plymouth County Sheriff's Department identified it as a known green meteor, and the case was closed without further investigation.

On 1 February 2023 at 5:45 AM Central time, a concerned citizen contacted the FAA to report seeing something green, roughly the size of a car, falling from the sky near Merrill, Iowa. The report was logged by the FAA Central Regional Operations Center and routed through the FAA Washington Operations Center.

The FAA relayed the report to the Plymouth County Sheriff's Department. The Sheriff's Department responded that there was a known green meteor flying over the sky during the reported time, effectively closing the matter.

No photographs, radar data, or other sensor evidence accompanied the report. There is no record of a direct witness interview or any follow-up investigation beyond the Sheriff's Department's identification of the meteor.

The document itself is a brief incident log rather than a detailed investigation file. It contains geographic coordinates (approximately 42.72°N, 96.25°W), the time of the report, and the law enforcement resolution. Redaction levels are low — the record appears to be released largely intact.
