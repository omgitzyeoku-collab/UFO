# Alaska Airline Crew Reported Stationary Black Object at 16,000 Feet

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA preliminary incident report documents a UAP sighting by the crew of Ravn Alaska Flight 140 on 20 February 2023. While flying at 6,000 feet near Kenai, Alaska, the crew observed a round black object hovering at approximately 16,000 feet; no evasive action was taken and Alaska NORAD Region was notified.

This document is a preliminary incident report from the FAA Washington Operations Center, filed on 20 February 2023 at 6:59 PM Alaska Standard Time. It records a UAP (unidentified anomalous phenomena) sighting reported by a commercial flight crew in Alaskan airspace.

Ravn Alaska Flight 140, a De Havilland DH8A turboprop flying from Anchorage to Kenai, was heading southwest at 6,000 feet, approximately 52 nautical miles northwest of Kenai. The crew reported observing a round black object that appeared stationary at roughly 16,000 feet — about 10,000 feet above their own altitude. No evasive action was taken.

Anchorage ARTCC (Air Route Traffic Control Center) was advised, and the Alaska NORAD Region — the military command responsible for North American aerospace defence in that area — was notified. The report does not mention any radar contact or sensor data corroborating the visual sighting.

This is a preliminary report only. No follow-up investigation, identification, or resolution is documented in this record. The object remains unidentified based on the information available here.
