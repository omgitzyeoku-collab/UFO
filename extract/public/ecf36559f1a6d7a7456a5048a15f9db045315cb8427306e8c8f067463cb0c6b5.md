# FAA logged two unexplained flashing lights near Colorado Springs in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report from March 21, 2023, documenting two flashing lights spotted traveling together about 12 nautical miles northeast of Colorado Springs Airport. Neither the local tower nor the Denver regional radar centre could identify any known aircraft in the area, and the case was closed without explanation.

On March 21, 2023, at 09:24, the air traffic control tower at Colorado Springs Airport (COS) filed a SKYWATCH incident report — a standardised FAA form used to log unidentified aerial phenomena. Controllers observed what they described as two flashing lights traveling together, roughly 12 nautical miles northeast of the airport, at an unknown altitude.

COS contacted Denver Center (ZDV), the regional radar facility responsible for that airspace, to check whether any known aircraft were in the area. Neither facility could account for the lights. The report notes simply: "No air traffic in the area. ZDV reported they had no traffic in the area either."

The report was flagged under the primary code "Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon" and marked as paged, meaning it was escalated through the FAA's internal notification process. Despite this, the case status is listed as closed. No resolution, identification, or follow-up investigation is described in the document.

The record contains no radar data, no photographs, and no measurements of the object's speed, size, or altitude beyond "unknown." It is a single-page administrative log rather than an in-depth investigation file.
