# Pilot Reports Three Stationary Flashing Lights at 36,000 Feet

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA SKYWATCH report documents a general aviation pilot's sighting of three lights turning on and off above the horizon while flying at 36,000 feet over the ocean southeast of the Bahamas on 6 August 2022. The case was closed with no explanation or investigation findings included in the document.

This document is an FAA (Federal Aviation Administration) incident report filed under the agency's SKYWATCH system for tracking unidentified aerial phenomena. It records a sighting by the crew of aircraft N765QS, a general aviation flight travelling northwest at 36,000 feet on a route from Providenciales (Turks and Caicos) to Miami.

On 6 August 2022 at 03:48, the crew reported seeing three lights off the right side of the aircraft, approximately 115 nautical miles southeast of the ZQA navigation point. The lights were described as stationary, positioned above the horizon, and turning on and off.

The FAA notified the UFO reporting centre of the incident. The case status is marked as "Closed," but the document contains no investigation findings, analysis, or conclusion about what the lights were.

The report is brief — essentially a single-page intake form capturing the initial sighting details. No radar data, no corroborating witness statements, and no follow-up analysis appear in the released document. The "Closed" status indicates the case was administratively shut, not that an explanation was reached.
