# Spirit Airlines crew reported unidentified object at 37,000 feet over Mississippi

**FAA · 2023**

> An FAA incident report documents the crew of Spirit Airlines flight 797 spotting a black-and-white object roughly the size of a small aircraft while cruising at 37,000 feet near Picayune, Mississippi on August 21, 2023. The crew took no evasive action, and the report contains no identification of the object or follow-up investigation results.

This document is an FAA Washington Operations Center incident report filed on August 21, 2023. It records a pilot-reported sighting of an unidentified anomalous phenomenon — the term the US government now uses instead of "UFO."

The crew of Spirit Airlines flight 797, an Airbus A320neo travelling from Houston (IAH) to Fort Lauderdale (FLL), reported the sighting at 0727 Central time while flying eastbound at flight level 370 — roughly 37,000 feet. The object was observed approximately 11 nautical miles southwest of Picayune, Mississippi, off the left side of the aircraft. They described it as black and white and about the size of a small aircraft.

The crew did not take evasive action and no law enforcement was notified. Houston ARTCC (Air Route Traffic Control Center) — the regional air traffic control facility — was advised of the sighting.

The report is marked as preliminary and contains no resolution: the object was never identified. There is no radar data, sensor evidence, or follow-up investigation included in the document. The FAA did not reach a conclusion about what the crew saw.

## What to know

- This is a single-source pilot report with no corroborating radar data or other sensor evidence included in the document.
- The report is marked preliminary and may not represent a final FAA assessment.
- No follow-up investigation results are included — the object was never identified or explained.
- The crew observed the object visually; no instrument readings or photographs are referenced.
- The document shows low evidence of redaction, meaning what is published appears to be close to the full report.
