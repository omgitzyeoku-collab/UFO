# Gemini 5 Crew Reported Debris and Glittering Objects in Orbit

**NASA · August 30 - September 2, 1965 · Cape Kennedy, Florida**

> This 1965 NASA transcript records astronauts Gordon Cooper and Pete Conrad describing debris, 'snow,' and 'glittering pieces' observed during the Gemini 5 mission. In late August 1965, NASA conducted the Gemini 5 mission — the fifth crewed flight in its Gemini programme. After the mission, astronauts L. Gordon Cooper and Charles 'Pete' Conrad sat down for a technical debriefing at Crew Quarters, Cape Kennedy, Florida, between August 30 and September 2, 1965. This document is Part II of the preliminary transcript from that debriefing, derived from voice recordings of the crew.

The transcript includes a section on 'Visual Sightings' spanning pages 157 to 220 — a substantial portion of the document. According to the metadata description, Cooper and Conrad described observing debris, 'snow,' and 'all sorts of glittering pieces of this, that and the other thing' during the flight.

It is worth noting that such observations are not unusual in spaceflight. Spacecraft outgassing, insulation fragments, and ice crystals are common sources of visible particles outside a capsule window. The document metadata does not characterise these sightings as anomalous or unexplained.
