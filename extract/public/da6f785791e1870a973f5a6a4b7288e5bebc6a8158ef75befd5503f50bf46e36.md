**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA SKYWATCH incident report documents a February 2007 event in which at least 12 aircraft visually observed an unidentified object between 34,000 and 40,000 feet near Hayden, Colorado. The object produced no radar return and appeared to be tracking the aircraft. The case was closed with no explanation provided.
