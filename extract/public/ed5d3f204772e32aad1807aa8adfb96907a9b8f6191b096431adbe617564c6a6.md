# Three Aircraft Crews Reported Hovering Lights Near Grand Turk

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a May 2024 sighting in which three separate aircraft crews independently reported a group of hovering lights approximately 20 nautical miles north-northwest of Grand Turk island. The lights were not detected on air traffic control radar, and the case was closed without explanation.

On 26 May 2024 at 04:19 UTC, the crew of a Gulfstream V business jet (callsign N513MA) reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon while flying at 43,000 feet, roughly 10 nautical miles south-southwest of Grand Turk in the Turks and Caicos Islands. The aircraft was operating as a general aviation flight from Piarco International Airport in Trinidad to Miami.

The crew described a group of hovering lights approximately 20 nautical miles north-northwest of Grand Turk. Two other aircraft — Frontier flight FFT1745 (an Airbus A320neo) and JetBlue flight JBU2054 (an Airbus A320) — independently filed similar reports of the same phenomenon.

Miami Air Route Traffic Control Center (ZMA), the FAA facility handling the airspace, checked its radar systems. The lights were not detected on radar despite being visually observed by all three crews.

The report was filed through the FAA's internal SKYWATCH reporting system, which is used to log unidentified aerial phenomena. The case is marked as closed. No resolution, identification, or further investigation details are included in the document.

No operator or tail number is listed for the primary reporting aircraft beyond its callsign, and no additional details about the nature or behaviour of the lights are provided.
