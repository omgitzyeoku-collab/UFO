# United Airlines Crew Reported Three Blinking Lights Over Nebraska in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA preliminary report documents a sighting by the crew of United Airlines flight 476 on 4 October 2023. While cruising at roughly 35,000 feet over western Nebraska, the crew observed three stationary bright white lights blinking in a sequential pattern off the left side of the aircraft. No evasive action was taken and no investigation results are included.

This document is a preliminary information report from the FAA's Washington Operations Center. It records a UAP (unidentified aerial phenomenon) sighting reported by a commercial airline crew — not a completed investigation or any kind of conclusion about what was seen.

On 4 October 2023 at 03:25 MDT, the crew of United Airlines flight 476 — a Boeing 737-900 flying from San Francisco to Chicago O'Hare — spotted something unusual while at flight level 350, approximately 35,000 feet. The sighting occurred about 42 nautical miles northeast of Scottsbluff, Nebraska, at coordinates 42.3°N, 103.0°W. The crew described three stationary bright white lights blinking in a left-to-right and right-to-left pattern, visible from the left side of the aircraft.

The crew did not take evasive action, and no law enforcement was notified. The report was relayed through Denver ARTCC (Air Route Traffic Control Center) to FAA operations.

The document contains no follow-up analysis, no radar data, no photographs, and no determination of what the lights were. It is a single-page preliminary record — essentially a logbook entry that an unusual sighting was reported through official channels.
