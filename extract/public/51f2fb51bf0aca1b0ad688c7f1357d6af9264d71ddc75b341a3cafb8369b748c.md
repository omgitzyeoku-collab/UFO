# AARO Org Chart Shows Office Structure and Leadership as of August 2024

**AARO**

> This document is an internal organizational chart for the All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO), the Pentagon's UFO-investigation office, dated August 2024. It names the director and deputy director, lists the office's divisions, and shows staffing categories — but nearly all individual staff names are blacked out.

This record, released under a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) request, is not a UFO sighting report. It is an organizational chart — essentially a staffing diagram — for the All-Domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO), which sits within the Office of the Under Secretary of Defense for Intelligence and Security. The chart is dated August 2024.

The two most senior leaders are named: Director Jon Kosloski, a Senior Executive Service (SES) official, and Deputy Director Tim Phillips, detailed from the Office of the Director of National Intelligence's Special National Intelligence Service (ODNI-SNIS). Beyond those two, nearly every other staff member's name is redacted under FOIA exemption (b)(6), which protects personal privacy.

The chart shows AARO organized into several divisions: Analysis, Operations, Science and Technology, Strategic Communications, and a Chief of Staff function. It also lists a Federally Funded Research and Development Center (FFRDC) component and liaison officers from NASA, the Army, and the Navy. Specific roles mentioned include radar analysts, all-source intelligence analysts, a production manager, operations specialists, and a Pentagon Force Protection Agency (PFPA) detailee. A special assistant role for data architecture is also listed.

Staffing categories shown in the chart's legend include liaison officers (LNOs), Intelligence and Security cadre, surge staff, Joint Operations Assignment or Reservist personnel, and detailees from other agencies. This mix suggests AARO drew on both permanent staff and temporary personnel from across the defense and intelligence community.

Because this is an administrative document, it contains no information about any specific UAP sighting, investigation, or conclusion. Its value is in showing how the office was structured and resourced at a particular point in time.

## What to know

- This is an org chart, not a UAP incident report — it contains zero data about any sighting or investigation.
- Heavy redactions under FOIA exemption (b)(6) obscure all staff names except the Director and Deputy Director, making it impossible to fully assess AARO's staffing level or expertise.
- The chart reflects AARO's structure as of August 2024 and may not match current staffing or organization.
- Some position titles may be slightly garbled due to OCR artifacts from the PDF scan, so exact wording of certain role names should be treated with minor caution.
- The document is single-source — it shows what AARO chose to depict in its own internal chart, with no independent verification of whether all listed positions were actually filled.
