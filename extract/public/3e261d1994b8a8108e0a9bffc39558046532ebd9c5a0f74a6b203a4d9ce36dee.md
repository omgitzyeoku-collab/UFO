# American Airlines Crew Reports Formation of Coloured Lights Near Nashville

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA preliminary incident report documents a sighting by the crew of American Airlines flight 2399 on 1 February 2023. While flying at roughly 35,000 feet near Nashville, Tennessee, the crew observed red, green, and white lights travelling in formation and changing colour and brightness. No evasive action was taken and no identification of the phenomenon was made.

This document is a preliminary incident report from the FAA's Washington Operations Center. It records a sighting reported by the flight crew of American Airlines flight 2399, an Airbus A321neo flying from Las Vegas to Charlotte, on 1 February 2023 at 04:07 Central Standard Time.

While eastbound at flight level 350 (approximately 35,000 feet), roughly 10 nautical miles west of Nashville, the crew observed an unidentified aerial phenomenon to the left side of the aircraft. They described it as red, green, and white lights travelling in formation, changing in colour and brightness. The crew did not take evasive action.

Memphis Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) was advised of the sighting, and Bowling Green Police Department in Kentucky was also notified. The report includes coordinates (36.12°N, 86.86°W) consistent with the area west of Nashville.

This is a preliminary report only. The document does not contain any concluded investigation, identification of the phenomenon, or follow-up findings. There is no corroborating radar data or additional witness testimony included. The level of redaction in the document is low.
