# United Airlines Crew Reports White-Blue Object Near San Antonio

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA operations center log records a sighting by the crew of United Airlines Flight 1715 on 13 January 2024. While flying an Airbus A320 from San Antonio to Denver at 7,000 feet, the crew observed a white and blue unidentified aerial phenomenon off the right side of the aircraft — no evasive action was taken and no law enforcement was notified.

This document is a preliminary incident log from the FAA (Federal Aviation Administration) Washington Operations Center. It records a UAP (unidentified aerial phenomenon) sighting reported by a commercial airline crew — not a full investigation or conclusion.

On 13 January 2024 at approximately 2:26 p.m. Central time, the crew of United Airlines Flight 1715 — an Airbus A320 flying from San Antonio, Texas to Denver, Colorado — reported seeing a white and blue unidentified aerial phenomenon off the right side of their aircraft. The plane was eastbound at 7,000 feet, roughly 16 nautical miles southeast of San Antonio. San Antonio TRACON (Terminal Radar Approach Control), the local air traffic facility, was advised of the sighting.

The crew did not take evasive action, and no law enforcement was notified. The record contains coordinates placing the sighting near latitude 29.37°N, longitude 98.28°W, consistent with the area southeast of San Antonio.

The document ends there. No follow-up investigation, resolution, or identification of the object is included in this record. There is no radar data, no photographs, and no additional witness accounts. This is a single log entry capturing what the crew reported, and nothing more.
