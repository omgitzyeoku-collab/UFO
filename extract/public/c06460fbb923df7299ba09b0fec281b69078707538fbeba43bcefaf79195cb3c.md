# Airline Crew's UFO Report Resolved as Misidentified Southwest Airlines Flight

**FAA · 2023**

> An FAA incident report from February 2017 documents a regional airline crew reporting a white object descending alongside their aircraft near New York. Air traffic control identified the object as a Southwest Airlines Boeing 737 departing Philadelphia roughly 20 nautical miles away, and the case was closed with no further action.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report filed under record number 237UAP00030. It records an event on 6 February 2017 at 23:49 UTC, reported through New York Center (the FAA facility designated ZNY). The report was exported by Nicholas D. Torgerson and carries the primary code "OTHER."

The crew of flight AWI3851, operating a Canadair Challenger 800 regional jet (aircraft type CRJ2) for carrier AWI, reported a white object off the right side of their aircraft. They described the object as descending alongside them and following the aircraft, but said they were unsure what it was.

FAA headquarters in Denver (DEN) contacted the Potomac TRACON (PCT) to get more details from the crew. After air traffic control pointed out a nearby aircraft on a parallel track — Southwest Airlines flight SWA814, a Boeing 737 departing Philadelphia — the crew assessed that the object was most likely that aircraft, roughly 20 nautical miles away, positioned over the horizon and into the sun. The sun's position appears to have contributed to the initial misidentification.

The case was closed with no further action (NFA). This is a single-page administrative export, not a detailed investigation report. The document shows minimal redaction.
