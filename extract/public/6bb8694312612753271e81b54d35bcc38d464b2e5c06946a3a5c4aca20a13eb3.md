# Multiple Pilots Reported Static Orange Lights at High Altitude

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents multiple aircraft reporting stationary orange flashing lights approximately 25 nautical miles southwest of GFS, at altitudes between 26,000 and 32,000 feet, on 26 January 2024. The lights were not detected on air traffic control radar, and the case was closed without explanation.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report — a standardised form the Federal Aviation Administration uses to log pilot reports of unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP). It was filed on 26 January 2024 at 06:49, under the identifier 237UAP00345, and processed through the Denver (DEN) office.

According to the report, multiple aircraft heading eastbound at various flight levels observed orange flashing lights approximately 25 nautical miles southwest of GFS. The lights were described as "static in nature" — meaning they appeared stationary rather than moving — and were observed at altitudes between roughly 26,000 and 32,000 feet (FL260 to FL320).

Notably, the phenomenon did not appear on the air traffic control facility's radar system. This means whatever the pilots saw left no radar signature, which could indicate a non-solid object, atmospheric phenomenon, or simply a limitation of the radar equipment in that area.

The report's status is listed as "Closed." No resolution, identification, or explanation is provided in the document. The FAA did not attribute the sighting to any known aircraft, weather event, or other cause.
