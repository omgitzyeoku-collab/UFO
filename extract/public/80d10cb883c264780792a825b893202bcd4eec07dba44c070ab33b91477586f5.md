# United Airlines Crew Reported Large Balloon at 39,000 Feet Near Chicago

**FAA · 2023**

> An FAA incident report documents a United Airlines Boeing 737-800 crew observing what they described as a large balloon while flying at 39,000 feet near Chicago on 3 February 2023. The FAA classified the object as an Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon and closed the case without providing resolution details.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report — a standard form the Federal Aviation Administration uses to log pilot observations of unusual objects in US airspace. It records an event on 3 February 2023 involving United Airlines flight 532, a Boeing 737-800 flying a commercial route from Denver (DEN) to Chicago O'Hare (ORD).

According to the report, the flight crew reported passing under a large balloon while heading eastbound at flight level 390 — roughly 39,000 feet. The observation occurred approximately 20 nautical miles east of Chicago Executive Airport (PWK), placing it in the greater Chicago area.

Despite the crew describing the object as a large balloon, the FAA classified the incident under its primary code for Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon. This classification indicates that no formal identification of the object was confirmed through the FAA's reporting process.

The case is marked as closed. The document includes no resolution details, no corroborating radar data, and no imagery. It is a single-source pilot report — one crew's account filed through standard FAA channels.

The report shows minimal redaction. What is notable is not what was hidden but what is absent: there is no follow-up investigation documented, no explanation of what the object was, and no indication that the FAA pursued further identification.

## What to know

- The case is marked closed with no resolution details provided, so it is unknown whether the object was ever identified.
