# Air Force F-35 Pilot Reported UAP Near Luke Base in Arizona

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA Washington Operations Center notification from March 8, 2023, recording that a US Air Force F-35 pilot observed an unidentified anomalous phenomenon (UAP) while flying near Luke Air Force Base in Arizona. The document is a brief incident alert — not an investigation — and contains no description of the object or any conclusions about what it was.

On March 8, 2023, a US Air Force F-35 fighter jet pilot reported seeing a UAP while flying southbound at approximately 19,000 feet, about 65 nautical miles west of Luke Air Force Base in Arizona. The sighting was logged by the FAA's Washington Operations Center as an incident notification.

The Albuquerque Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) — the FAA facility managing high-altitude airspace in the region — advised the Air Force unit designated USAF SWROD2 about the incident. The La Paz County Sheriff's office was also notified. According to the document, no evasive action was reported by the pilot.

The document provides coordinates placing the incident in a remote area of western Arizona, consistent with airspace west of Luke AFB. Beyond these operational details, the notification contains no description of what the pilot saw — no size, shape, speed, or behaviour of the object is recorded.

This is a preliminary notification only. The FAA did not include any follow-up investigation, analysis, or conclusions in this document. It exists as a record that the sighting occurred and that the relevant authorities were informed.
