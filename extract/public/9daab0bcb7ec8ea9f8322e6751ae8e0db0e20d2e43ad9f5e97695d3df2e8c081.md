# Commercial Pilot Reports Blinking Lights Above 39,000 Feet Near Little Rock

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a sighting on 19 August 2022 in which a commercial Boeing 767 crew and multiple other aircraft observed blinking lights at high altitude near Little Rock, Arkansas. NORAD later reported that Starlink satellites were in the area, and the case was closed.

This is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report filed under case number 237UAP00107, categorised as an Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon. It documents an event that took place on 19 August 2022 at 05:50 UTC.

A commercial Boeing 767, callsign GTI120, was flying northeast at flight level 390 (approximately 39,000 feet) in the vicinity of Little Rock (LIT) when the crew reported seeing 2 and 3 blinking lights at an altitude well above their own, holding steady over Little Rock. The crew were not the only witnesses — multiple aircraft observed the same lights.

NORAD (the North American Aerospace Defense Command) subsequently reported that Starlink satellites were in the area at the time. The case was marked as closed, indicating no further investigation was deemed necessary.

The report is a single-page summary with limited detail. It does not describe how long the observation lasted, the exact positions of the lights, or whether the Starlink explanation was formally accepted as the cause. Redaction levels are low — the document appears to be released largely intact.
