# FAA Report: Green Fireball Seen Near Memphis in November 2021

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report documenting a large green fireball observed by Harding County residents and independently by an off-duty air traffic controller approximately 82 nautical miles east of Memphis on 20 November 2021. The case was closed with no identification or resolution noted.

This document is a single-page FAA SKYWATCH incident report filed under the classification "Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon." It describes an event that took place at approximately 06:10 on 20 November 2021, roughly 82 nautical miles east of Memphis.

Residents of Harding County reported a large green fireball to the Harding County Sheriff. They described it as travelling like a shooting star. Separately, a controller at the Memphis Air Route Traffic Control Centre (ZME) observed the same phenomenon while on break, providing an independent second account.

The FAA's Airway Operations (AWO) office was notified. The report was flagged as paged, meaning it triggered an alert within the FAA's notification system. Despite this, the document is only a single page and contains no references to radar data, instrument recordings, or any follow-up investigation.

The case status is listed as "Closed," but no resolution or identification of the object is provided. The report does not conclude what the phenomenon was — it simply records the observations and closes the file.

The description of a fast-moving green fireball is consistent with what astronomers would call a meteor or bolide, though the FAA report itself does not offer that or any other explanation.
