# FAA Tower Staff Spotted Five or More Lights Over Montana

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA preliminary report documents a sighting on 2 December 2023 in which air traffic controllers at Great Falls International Airport in Montana observed five or more unidentified lights at roughly 40,000 feet to the northeast. No pilots reported seeing the lights, and there was no disruption to air traffic operations.

On 2 December 2023, staff in the air traffic control tower at Great Falls International Airport (GTF) in Montana observed an unidentified aerial phenomenon — five or more lights positioned to the northeast of the airport at flight level 400, which corresponds to approximately 40,000 feet. The tower reported the sighting to the FAA Washington Operations Center, which logged it as a preliminary information report.

No aircraft in the area independently reported seeing the lights. The distance between the airport and the phenomenon could not be determined, which means the altitude estimate of 40,000 feet is based on visual observation alone rather than radar confirmation. The FAA noted no impact to air traffic control operations as a result of the sighting.

The document is a preliminary report, not a completed investigation or conclusion. It records what the tower staff observed and confirms that no further disruption occurred. There is no indication of follow-up analysis, radar data, or an explanation for what the lights were.
