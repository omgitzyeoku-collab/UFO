# Airline Crew and Other Pilots Reported White Lights Over Oklahoma

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA preliminary report documents a sighting by the crew of United Airlines flight 2484 on 17 February 2024, in which 1–5 white lights of varying brightness were observed moving vertically and horizontally at 37,000 feet near Ardmore, Oklahoma. Other eastbound aircraft at similar altitudes independently reported the same phenomenon.

On 17 February 2024 at approximately 3:54 AM Central time, the crew of United Airlines flight 2484 — a Boeing 737 MAX 8 flying from Phoenix to Newark — reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon to Fort Worth Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC). The aircraft was eastbound at 37,000 feet, roughly 20 nautical miles north of Ardmore, Oklahoma.

The crew described the UAP as between one and five white lights of differing brightness. The lights moved both vertically and horizontally and would appear and then quickly disappear. No onboard sensor data is referenced in the report; the observations are entirely visual from the cockpit.

Other eastbound aircraft flying between 35,000 and 37,000 feet also reported seeing the same phenomenon, though the report does not name or count those corroborating aircraft.

The document is a preliminary information report from the FAA's Washington Operations Center, filed under reference WOC 7-3333. It records the initial sighting details but does not contain any conclusions, identification of the lights, or evidence of further investigation. There is little sign of redaction in the document.

## What to know

- This is a preliminary report, not a final investigation or finding. It records what was reported, not what was concluded.
- All observations are visual from cockpit crews. The report contains no radar data, infrared imagery, or other sensor evidence.
- The corroborating aircraft are not named or counted — the report says only that other eastbound traffic reported the same thing.
- The document offers no explanation or identification of the lights. It neither rules in nor rules out any specific cause.
- The incident date in the metadata says 2023, but the report itself is dated 17 February 2024 — readers should note this discrepancy.
