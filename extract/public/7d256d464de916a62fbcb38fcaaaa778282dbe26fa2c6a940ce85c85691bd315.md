# Jordanian Airline Crew Reported Two Bright White Lights Near Chicago

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA operations center report documents a UAP sighting by the crew of Royal Jordanian Airlines flight 264 on 1 March 2023. While flying a Boeing 787-8 from Chicago O'Hare to Amman, Jordan, the crew reported two bright white lights at approximately 18,000 feet, 25 nautical miles north of O'Hare. No evasive action was taken.

This document is a log entry from the FAA's Washington Operations Center recording a UAP (unidentified anomalous phenomena) sighting reported by a commercial airline crew on 1 March 2023 at 7:30 PM Central time.

The crew of Royal Jordanian Airlines flight 264, operating a Boeing 787-8 on a route from Chicago O'Hare to Amman, Jordan, reported observing two bright white lights. The aircraft was heading northbound at flight level 180 (roughly 18,000 feet altitude), approximately 25 nautical miles north of O'Hare International Airport. The recorded coordinates place the sighting in the Chicago, Illinois area.

Chicago Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) was advised of the report. The crew took no evasive action, and no law enforcement notification was made.

The document is a preliminary operations center log entry — not a full investigation or analytical report. It contains no description of the lights' behaviour, movement, size, or how long they were visible. There is no indication that radar data or additional witnesses corroborated the sighting.
