# Delta Crew Reports Large Balloon at 50,000 Feet in February 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA SKYWATCH incident report documents a Delta Air Lines crew observing a large balloon at approximately 50,000 feet while flying at 36,000 feet on 3 February 2023. The report was closed with no identification of the object.

On 3 February 2023 at 17:22 UTC, the crew of Delta flight DAL1059 reported seeing a large balloon while flying southwest-bound at FL360 (approximately 36,000 feet). The object appeared off the right side of the aircraft and was estimated to be at FL500 (approximately 50,000 feet) or higher — well above the ceiling of most commercial aircraft.

The flight was a commercial Delta Air Lines service operating from Minneapolis (MSP) to Houston (IAH). The FAA logged the sighting under its SKYWATCH system with the primary code "Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon."

The report's status is marked as "Closed," but no resolution or identification of the object is recorded. There is no mention of corroborating radar data, photographs, or follow-up investigation in the document.

The date is notable: early February 2023 coincided with a wave of high-altitude balloon sightings across the United States, including the well-publicised Chinese surveillance balloon incident. This report may be connected to that broader episode, though the document itself does not draw that link.

## What to know

- This is a single-source observation from one flight crew. No radar data, imagery, or independent corroboration is included in the report.
- The altitude estimate of FL500 (around 50,000 feet) was made visually from a crew flying at 36,000 feet — such estimates are inherently approximate.
- The report is marked 'Closed' with no explanation of what the object was or whether any follow-up occurred.
- This sighting occurred during the February 2023 US balloon wave, so the object may have a mundane explanation not captured in this document.
