# AARO Deputy Director Biography Cleared for Public Release in 2023

**AARO**

> This document is an administrative clearance package authorizing the public release of the official biography of Timothy A. Phillips, who became Deputy Director of the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) in October 2023. It contains no information about UAP sightings or investigations — it is purely a personnel biography that was reviewed and approved for publication on the AARO website within two days.

This record is a DD Form 1910 — a standard Department of Defense form used to request permission before publishing official material. In this case, the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) submitted the biography of its new Deputy Director, Timothy A. Phillips, for security review on November 15, 2023. The biography was intended for publication on the aaro.mil website. The submission was handled by Paul M. Plescow of AARO.

The Defense Office of Prepublication and Security Review (DOPSR) received the request on November 16, 2023, and cleared it for open publication the following day, November 17, 2023, with no objection. The document was classified as unclassified throughout the process. The one-day turnaround is typical for routine, unclassified biography releases.

The biography itself describes Phillips as a Senior National Intelligence Service executive appointed in 2006, with over 40 years of government experience across various senior Intelligence Community assignments. He is also a retired United States Marine Corps Lieutenant Colonel who served in peacekeeping, humanitarian, or combat operations in Lebanon, Somalia, Kuwait, Iraq, Joint Task Force Bravo, and JTF 510. His education includes a Bachelor of Arts in Political Science from the University of Arizona, a Master's in Business and Information Resources Management from Webster University, and a Master of Military Science from Marine Corps University. He also served on the Council on Foreign Relations as a National Intelligence Fellow.

Several names, phone numbers, and email addresses are redacted throughout the document under the (b)(6) personal privacy exemption. These redactions apply to administrative contacts and reviewers, not to any substantive content about UAP topics.

This document contains no information about UAP sightings, encounters, investigations, or findings of any kind. It is entirely an administrative personnel record that was released as part of AARO's public-facing transparency efforts.
