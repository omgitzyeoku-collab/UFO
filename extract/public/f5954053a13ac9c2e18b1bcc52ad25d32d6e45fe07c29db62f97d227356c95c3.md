# Pilot Reports White Donut-Shaped Object Over West Texas

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA incident report from April 2023 in which a general aviation pilot reported seeing a white, donut-shaped object about 15 feet across while flying at 14,000 feet over west Texas. The case was logged in the FAA's SKYWATCH system and closed without explanation.

On 22 April 2023 at 23:20 UTC, a general aviation pilot flying a Cirrus S22T (callsign N781CW) reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon to Albuquerque Center (ZAB), the FAA facility handling that airspace. The aircraft was westbound at 14,000 feet, roughly 24 nautical miles northwest of Fort Stockton, Texas.

The pilot described the object as white and donut-shaped, approximately 15 feet in diameter. It was about 100 feet above the aircraft and appeared to be either stationary or moving in the opposite direction.

The sighting was logged under the FAA's SKYWATCH system with the identifier 237UAP00257 and assigned MOR ID ZAB-M-2023/04/22-0010. Law enforcement was not notified. The case status is marked as closed, with no resolution or explanation recorded in the document.

The report is a single-page FAA record. There is no indication of corroborating radar data, sensor readings, or additional witness accounts. No named individuals appear in the released document, and redaction levels are low — the record appears largely complete as released.
