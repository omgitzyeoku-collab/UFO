# Airline Crew Reports Five Unidentified Craft Over Utah at Extreme Altitude

**FAA · 2023**

> An FAA incident report documents the crew of Sun Country flight 3053, a Boeing 737-800, reporting five unidentified craft with white lights at an estimated 60,000–80,000 feet over eastern Utah on 4 February 2024. No radar returns corroborated the sighting.

This document is an FAA Washington Operations Center incident report filed on 4 February 2024 at 4:11 AM Mountain Standard Time. It records a UAP (unidentified aerial phenomenon) sighting by the flight crew of Sun Country flight 3053, a Boeing 737-800 operating between Portland, Oregon and Fort Worth Alliance, Texas.

While cruising southeast-bound at 41,000 feet, approximately 60 nautical miles southeast of the Myton VOR navigational beacon in Utah (coordinates 39.58°N, 109.27°W), the crew reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon off the left side of their aircraft. They described what appeared to be five separate craft with white lights travelling at altitudes between 60,000 and 80,000 feet — well above the service ceiling of virtually all known conventional aircraft.

Denver Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) was advised of the sighting. Radar operators found no primary targets corresponding to the reported objects.

The document contains no follow-up investigation, no resolution, and no agency conclusion about what the crew observed. It is a single incident report capturing the crew's account as relayed through air traffic control channels.

## What to know

- This is a single-source pilot report with no independent corroboration — no radar returns, no additional witnesses from other aircraft are mentioned.
- Altitude estimates of 60,000–80,000 feet were made visually from a distant aircraft at night; such estimates can be highly unreliable.
- The document contains no follow-up investigation or determination of what the objects were.
- There is minimal redaction in this record, but the absence of detail reflects the brevity of the report itself rather than deliberate withholding.
- The FAA drew no conclusions in this document — it is a raw incident log, not an analytical assessment.
