# FAA Report: Pilot Spots Red-Orange Lights at 16,000 Feet Over Alabama

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report documenting a sighting by a general aviation pilot on 29 December 2023. The pilot, flying southeast at 16,000 feet near coordinates 34°36'N 86°48'W (northern Alabama), reported red and orange lights moving in no specific direction. Air traffic control radar did not detect anything, and the case was closed without explanation.
