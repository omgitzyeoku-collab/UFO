# Delta 767 Crew Reported 'Jet Pack Man' Near LAX in 2022

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a January 2022 sighting by the crew of Delta flight DAL326, a Boeing 767 flying from New York to Los Angeles, who reported seeing what they described as a 'jet pack man' at roughly 2,400 feet while on approach near LAX. No other aircraft or police helicopters confirmed the sighting, and the FAA closed the case.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report filed under the classification "Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon." It records an event on 27 January 2022 at 00:50, involving Delta Air Lines flight DAL326, a Boeing 767 operating a commercial service from John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) in New York to Los Angeles International Airport (LAX).

While flying westbound at 2,200 feet, approximately 5 nautical miles east of LAX, the flight crew reported an unidentified object off the right side of the aircraft. They described it as a "jet pack man" at roughly 2,400 feet — about 200 feet above their own altitude.

The FAA's report notes that no other aircraft and no airborne police helicopter confirmed the sighting. The case was subsequently closed. The document gives no indication that any follow-up investigation was conducted or that the object was ever identified.

This report is one of several "jet pack man" sightings reported in the LAX area during this general period, though this particular document does not reference any of the others. The description "jet pack man" is the crew's own characterisation of what they saw, not a confirmed identification by the FAA or any other authority.

## What to know

- This is a single-source observation from one flight crew. No other aircraft or police helicopters corroborated the sighting.
- The term 'jet pack man' is the crew's description, not a confirmed identification of what the object was.
- The FAA closed the case with no stated resolution and no indication of further investigation.
- The document shows low evidence of redaction — what you see is essentially the full record.
