# FAA Logged Three Unidentified Lights Near Reagan Airport in 2024

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA notification documents a visual sighting of three unidentified lights flying at approximately 30,000 feet east of Reagan National Airport in Washington, DC, on February 4, 2024. The lights were not detected on air traffic control radar and no aircraft were involved.

On February 4, 2024, at 4:00 AM Eastern time, the FAA's Washington Operations Center logged a report from the National Air Traffic Control Tower (ATCT) describing an unidentified aerial phenomenon near Washington, DC. Controllers observed three lights flying at Flight Level 300 — roughly 30,000 feet — to the east of Reagan National Airport (DCA).

The report notes that the lights were not detected on air traffic control radar, making this a visual-only sighting from a single facility. No aircraft were involved in the incident, meaning no planes were endangered or required to take evasive action.

The document is classified as preliminary information from FAA Operations, referenced under number WOC 7-3333 RC/DD. It is not a final investigation report and does not offer any conclusion about what the lights were.

The coordinates listed in the report (38.907, -77.037) correspond to central Washington, DC, and indicate the general area rather than the precise position of the observed lights. There is minimal redaction in this document — the record is brief by nature, consisting of a standard operational notification rather than a detailed investigative file.
