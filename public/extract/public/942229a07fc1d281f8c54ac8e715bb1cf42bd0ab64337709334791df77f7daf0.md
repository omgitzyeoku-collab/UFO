# American Airlines Crew Reported Colored Lights Over Kansas at 37,000 Feet

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a sighting by the crew of American Airlines flight AAL661 on 17 December 2022, who observed red, green, and white lights fading in and out at high altitude while flying east of Kansas City at 37,000 feet. The report itself notes the lights were possibly satellites, and the case is closed.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report — the system the Federal Aviation Administration uses to log pilot reports of unidentified aerial phenomena. It covers a sighting reported by the crew of American Airlines flight AAL661, an Airbus A321 flying from Las Vegas to Philadelphia on 17 December 2022.

At 09:55 UTC, while eastbound at flight level 370 (37,000 feet) and approximately 95 nautical miles east of Kansas City, the crew reported seeing an unidentified aerial phenomenon. They described it as red, green, and white lights fading in and out at high altitude above them.

The report itself offers a possible explanation: satellites. No corroborating sensor data or additional witness accounts are documented in the record. The case has been closed by the FAA under reference number ZKC-M-2022/12/17-0001.

No tail number for the aircraft was recorded in the report, and no named individuals appear in the document. The incident location is not redacted but is described only by its position relative to Kansas City.

## What to know

- This is a single-source pilot observation with no corroborating radar, infrared, or other sensor data documented in the report.
- The report itself suggests a prosaic explanation — satellites — indicating the FAA did not treat this as a confirmed anomalous event.
- No aircraft tail number was recorded, which limits independent verification of the specific flight involved.
- The case is closed, meaning the FAA conducted no further investigation beyond this initial report.
- There is minimal redaction in this document; the record is short and largely complete, but also limited in detail.
