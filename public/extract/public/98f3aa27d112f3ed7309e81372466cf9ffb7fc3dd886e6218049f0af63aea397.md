# Delta Crew Reports Two Unidentified Lit Objects at High Altitude

**FAA · 2023**

> An FAA SKYWATCH incident report documents a Delta Air Lines crew on flight DAL726 observing what appeared to be two aircraft with red and white lights at very high altitude while flying at 39,000 feet on 14 January 2023. The case was closed with no explanation provided.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report filed under the primary code "Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon." It records an observation made by the crew of Delta flight DAL726, a commercial flight travelling from Kahului, Maui (OGG) to Atlanta (ATL), on 14 January 2023.

While flying eastbound at FL390 (39,000 feet) over the Razorback VOR (RZC), the crew reported seeing what appeared to be two aircraft with red and white lights at a very high altitude, off their 11 o'clock position. The sighting was reported through Fort Worth Center (ZFW), which is the FAA air traffic control facility covering that region. The FAA's Aviation Weather Office (AWO) was notified.

The report's status is listed as "Closed," but the document contains no resolution, follow-up findings, or explanation for what the crew observed. No corroborating radar data or sensor evidence is included. The tail number field for the reporting aircraft is blank, and no AWO response is documented.

The report is associated with the Denver (DEN) point of departure in the system, though the flight itself originated from Maui. Redaction levels in this document appear low — the report is brief rather than heavily censored.
