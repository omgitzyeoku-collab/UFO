# United Airlines Crew Reported Orbiting Lights Over Montana in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA preliminary notification records that the crew of United Airlines flight 1580, a Boeing 737-800 flying from Chicago to Vancouver, reported seeing orbiting lights from above while cruising at 38,000 feet near Billings, Montana on 3 February 2023. No evasive action was taken and no further investigation is documented here.

On the evening of 3 February 2023, the crew of United Airlines flight 1580 — a Boeing 737-800 en route from Chicago O'Hare to Vancouver, Canada — reported an unusual sighting to air traffic control. While flying west-northwest at 38,000 feet, roughly 60 nautical miles east of Billings, Montana, the crew observed what they described as orbiting lights from above.

The sighting was relayed by the Salt Lake City Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) to the FAA's Washington Operations Center, which logged it as a preliminary incident notification under reference 237UAP00453. The report notes that no evasive action was taken by the crew and no law enforcement notification was made.

This document is a brief operational log entry, not a full investigation report. It records only what the flight crew reported through standard air traffic control channels. There is no radar data, sensor corroboration, or follow-up analysis included. The notation "PRELIM INFO" on the document confirms it represents an initial record rather than a final assessment.

No conclusion is offered about what the crew saw. The FAA log simply documents the report as received — orbiting lights observed from above at cruise altitude over Montana — and leaves it at that.
