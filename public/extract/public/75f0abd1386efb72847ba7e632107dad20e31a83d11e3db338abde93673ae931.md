# Pilot Reports Three Lights in Triangle Formation Near Long Island Sound

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident notification records a pilot's report of three lights in a triangle formation maneuvering near Long Island Sound at roughly 20,000 feet on 15 November 2023. The document is a preliminary notification only — it contains no investigation, analysis, or explanation of what the lights were.

On 15 November 2023 at 03:46 Eastern time, the FAA's Washington Operations Center logged an incident notification about unidentified aerial phenomena. The report originated from the pilot of a Piper PA-28 light aircraft, registration N2166L, flying a route from University Park, Pennsylvania (UNV) to Lawrence, Massachusetts (LWM). Boston Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) relayed the report.

The pilot described three lights arranged in a triangle formation that were maneuvering in the vicinity of Long Island Sound. The observation was made at Flight Level 200 — approximately 20,000 feet. The coordinates logged in the report (41.50°N, 74.10°W) place the sighting over southeastern New York State, which is near but not directly over Long Island Sound. The pilot took no evasive action.

The document is a standard FAA operations-centre notification — essentially a intake form that records who reported what, when, and where. It does not contain any follow-up investigation, radar data, photographs, or conclusions about what the lights were. No additional witnesses or corroborating sensor data are referenced. There is little sign of redaction; the document simply contains very limited information by design, as it is a preliminary log entry rather than an analytical report.
