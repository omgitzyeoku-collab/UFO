# United Airlines Crew Reports Unexplained Lights Over South Dakota

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident notification records that the crew of United Airlines flight 2059, a Boeing 777-200 flying from San Francisco to Newark, reported unidentified lights appearing and disappearing at their altitude or higher on February 11, 2023. The sighting occurred near Sioux Falls, South Dakota, at 35,000–37,000 feet, and no investigation or explanation is documented.

This document is an FAA Washington Operations Center notification — essentially an internal log entry recording that a pilot crew reported something unusual. It is preliminary information, not an investigation report or a conclusion.

On February 11, 2023, at 03:32 CST, the crew of United Airlines flight 2059 reported lights "coming and going" at the same altitude or higher while flying eastbound between 35,000 and 37,000 feet. The aircraft, a Boeing 777-200 on the San Francisco-to-Newark route, was approximately 25 nautical miles south of Sioux Falls, South Dakota (coordinates 43.22°N, 96.74°W). Minneapolis Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) — the radar facility managing that airspace — relayed the report to FAA Operations.

The document notes that law enforcement was not notified. There is no record of follow-up investigation, corroborating radar data, additional witnesses, or any determination of what the lights were. The nature of the lights — whether aircraft, satellites, atmospheric phenomena, or something else — remains unresolved in this record.

The document shows minimal redaction. What it lacks is not hidden information but rather any investigative follow-up: this is where the official paper trail begins and, as far as this record shows, ends.
