# FBI Agents Surveyed a Prior UAP Sighting Site and Found Nothing Unusual

**FBI · December, 2024 · Northeastern United States**

> This FBI investigative report documents a December 2024 site survey of a location in the northeastern United States where two special agents had previously observed unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) in November 2024. The agents walked the area, found nothing unusual on the ground or in the trees, and judged that flying a drone there at night would be unrealistic due to tree cover.

This document is an FBI investigative activity report — specifically an FD-1057 form — describing a daytime site survey conducted in December 2024. Two FBI Special Agents returned to a location in the northeastern United States where they had observed UAP activity the previous month, in November 2024. The purpose of the visit was to photograph and survey the area.

The agents walked a roughly two-mile loop trail starting at approximately 10:30am, then left the trail to examine areas to the west and north. By about 11:00am they had descended to a water's edge on the northwest side, where they had an unobstructed line of sight south toward a property. They also had line of sight to multiple homes to the east. At the sites of the prior UAP observation, they found nothing unusual on the ground or at treetop level. They did find an old fire pit, but it showed no sign of recent use — the ground was covered in leaves.

The agents assessed that the tree cover throughout the area was moderate and concluded it would be unrealistic for someone to fly a drone or other craft at night without risking a crash into tree limbs. This assessment is the agents' subjective judgment, not a formal technical analysis.

The document is heavily redacted. Agent names, the specific location, property names, addresses, a nearby water body, and exact dates are all blacked out. No UAP was observed during this visit — the report describes only the physical environment of a prior sighting. The original UAP observation itself is described in separate companion documents, not in this one.

## What to know

- This is a follow-up site survey only — no UAP was observed during this visit. The document describes terrain, not a sighting.
- The report is heavily redacted: agent names, exact dates, location names, addresses, and a water body name are all withheld.
- The conclusion that drone flight would be unrealistic in the area is a subjective field judgment by the surveying agents, not a technical or engineering analysis.
- This document is part of a series (FBI-UAP-D004 through D008) from the same general area; companion documents and associated video files are not included here, so the full picture is incomplete.
- The original UAP observation referenced in this report is described in separate files — this document alone does not tell you what the agents saw in November 2024.
