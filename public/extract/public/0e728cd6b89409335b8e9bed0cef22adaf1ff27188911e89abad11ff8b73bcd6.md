# Commercial Pilot Reports Fast-Moving Orange Object Near Albany, NY

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a January 2024 sighting by the crew of a commercial Embraer E145 flight, who observed a bright orange object that repeatedly jumped between their cruising altitude (26,000 feet) and roughly 50,000 feet near Albany, New York. The case was closed with no explanation provided.

On 2 January 2024 at 01:43 UTC, the crew of commercial flight PDT5795 — an Embraer E145 travelling southwest at 26,000 feet — reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon roughly 25 nautical miles south-southwest of Albany, New York. The object was described as bright orange, moving quickly at near-level altitude before jumping to approximately 50,000 feet (FL500), then repeating the pattern.

The pilot continued to observe and report the same manoeuvres over a significant distance, all the way toward the DNY intersection. According to the report, some radar data was captured during the event until 01:55 UTC, though that radar data is not included in the document itself.

The FAA logged this under its SKYWATCH programme, which tracks unidentified aerial phenomena reported by pilots. The case was assigned the identifier 237UAP00336 and the mandatory occurrence report ID ZBW-M-2024/01/01-0002.

The case is marked as closed. No resolution, explanation, or follow-up findings are provided in the document. There is no indication of what the object was or whether any further investigation took place.

## What to know

- This is a single-source report from one flight crew. No corroborating witness accounts from other aircraft or ground observers are included.
- The altitude estimate of 50,000 feet is based on the pilot's visual judgement, not instrument measurement — visual altitude estimation at night can be unreliable.
- Radar data is referenced but not included in the document, so radar confirmation cannot be independently verified from this release alone.
- The case is marked 'Closed' with no stated conclusion — this does not mean the object was identified or that it remains unexplained; it simply means no outcome is recorded here.
- There is minimal redaction in this document, but minimal content overall — it is a short incident log, not a detailed investigation report.
