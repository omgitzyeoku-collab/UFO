# Delta Crew Reports Stationary Blinking Lights at 39,000 Feet Over Nebraska

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA incident report from October 2023 in which the crew of a Delta Air Lines Boeing 737 reported seeing three bright white stationary lights blinking in sequence while cruising at 39,000 feet northeast of Scottsbluff, Nebraska. The case was closed with no explanation or follow-up analysis included in the document.

On 4 October 2023, the crew of Delta Air Lines flight DAL2779 — a commercial Boeing 737-900 flying from Las Vegas to Minneapolis — reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon to the FAA. The sighting occurred at approximately 09:25 UTC while the aircraft was cruising at flight level 390, roughly 39,000 feet.

The crew described three bright white lights off the left side of the aircraft, about 83 nautical miles northeast of Scottsbluff, Nebraska. The lights were stationary, not changing altitude, and were blinking sequentially from left to right and then right to left. A Mandatory Occurrence Report was initiated through the FAA's Denver facility.

The case is marked as closed. The document provides no resolution, no identification of what the lights were, and no indication that further investigation was conducted. There is no radar data, imagery, or additional witness testimony included.

This is a single-page FAA SKYWATCH report — a standardised form for logging pilot-reported aerial incidents. It records what the crew saw and basic flight details, but it is not an analytical document and draws no conclusions.

## What to know
