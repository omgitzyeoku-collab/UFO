# UPS Cargo Pilot Reports Torpedo-Shaped Object Near Palm Springs

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a UPS cargo pilot's sighting of a fast-moving, white, torpedo-shaped object that passed roughly 500 feet below the aircraft near Palm Springs, California on 18 May 2021. The pilot explicitly ruled out a drone, no evasive action was taken, and the National UFO Reporting Office was notified.

This document is a preliminary incident report from the FAA's Washington Operations Center. It records an observation made by the pilot of UPS flight 913, a Boeing 757-200 cargo aircraft, while flying eastbound at approximately 24,000 feet, 29 miles west of Palm Springs, California on 18 May 2021.

According to the report, the pilot observed a white, torpedo-shaped object pass roughly 500 feet below the aircraft's left wing. The object was described as fast-moving. The pilot explicitly stated it was not a UAS (unmanned aerial system, commonly called a drone). No evasive action was taken.

SoCal TRACON — the regional air traffic control facility — was advised of the sighting, and the National UFO Reporting Office was notified. The report is marked as preliminary information and was filed as a late report.

The document contains no follow-up investigation, no radar corroboration, and no conclusion about what the object was. It is a single-source account based entirely on the pilot's observation. Notably, the metadata lists the incident year as 2023, but the document itself clearly dates the event to 18 May 2021.
