# Delta Pilot Reported Two Bright Lights in Formation Near Virginia

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA SKYWATCH incident report documents a Delta Air Lines pilot's sighting of two bright lights flying in apparent formation between Norfolk and Richmond, Virginia. The pilot reported the objects appeared to reverse course and fly toward the aircraft before turning around. The case is closed with no explanation offered.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report filed under record number 237UAP00047. It records a sighting reported by the pilot-in-command of Delta Air Lines flight 1748, an Airbus A320 operating a commercial route from Fort Lauderdale (FLL) to LaGuardia (LGA). The report was handled by Washington Center (ZDC).

At 0539Z, the pilot reported seeing two bright lights between Norfolk (ORF) and Richmond (RIC) that appeared to be flying in formation, heading northeast. According to the report, the formation then appeared to reverse course and fly toward the aircraft before turning around again.

The FAA notified a UFO reporting centre and an entity abbreviated as "JH" (which is not explained in the document). The report was filed under the primary code "OTHER" rather than a specific UAP category, and its status is listed as closed. No conclusions about the identity of the objects are recorded.

No radar data, sensor readings, or additional witness accounts are included in the report. The entire account rests on the pilot's visual observation alone.
