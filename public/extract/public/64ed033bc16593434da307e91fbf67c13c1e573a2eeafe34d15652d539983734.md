# Airline Crew Reported Unidentified Object at 11,300 Feet Over New Jersey

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a July 2019 sighting by the crew of a commercial regional jet (Endeavor 3468) who observed a large unidentified object at 11,300 feet near Robbinsville, New Jersey. No description of the object was provided, no evasive action was taken, and law enforcement was not notified.

This document is an FAA Washington Operations Center incident report from July 13, 2019. It records that the crew of Endeavor 3468 — a CRJ-900 regional jet flying from Asheville, North Carolina to LaGuardia Airport in New York — reported seeing a large unidentified aerial object while in flight.

The crew described the object as a large UAS (unmanned aircraft system) or "UFO" at 11,300 feet, located roughly 10 miles southwest of Robbinsville, New Jersey. The sighting was relayed from New York TRACON (Terminal Radar Approach Control) to Washington ARTCC (Air Route Traffic Control Center). The report was filed late, with the observation itself occurring at approximately 2:05 PM Eastern time.

Notably, the crew did not provide any physical description of the object — its shape, colour, size, or direction of flight are absent from the record. No evasive action was taken by the aircraft, and law enforcement agencies were not notified.

The FAA report does not draw any conclusion about what the object was. It is a factual log of what the crew reported, with no follow-up investigation or resolution documented here. The metadata lists a 2023 date, but the source text clearly places the incident on July 13, 2019 — the later date likely reflects when the document was processed for public release.
