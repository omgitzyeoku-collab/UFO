# Pilot Reports Fast-Climbing UFO During Night Flight Over South Carolina

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report documenting a UFO sighting by the pilot of a small turboprop aircraft on 13 February 2021. The pilot, flying at 18,000 feet on a route from Charleston to Evansville, observed a fast-moving, climbing object off the right side of the aircraft near Anderson, South Carolina. The report was closed with no indication of follow-up findings.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report — a standardised form the Federal Aviation Administration uses to log unusual aerial sightings reported by pilots and air traffic controllers. It was filed on 13 February 2021 at 23:39 UTC under reference number 237UAP00045.

At 23:15 that evening, the pilot in command of a TBM8 (a single-engine turboprop) with callsign N14SC was flying northwest-bound at flight level 180 — roughly 18,000 feet — on a general aviation route from Charleston Executive Airport (JZI) to Evansville Regional Airport (EVV). The pilot reported observing an unidentified object positioned at the Anderson VOR (a ground-based navigation beacon in South Carolina), 060-degree radial at 8 nautical miles, off the right side of the aircraft. The object was described as fast-moving and climbing.

The FAA logged the sighting and a message was left with the UFO reporting centre. The report is marked as "Closed" with no further detail on any investigation, resolution, or identification of the object.

Notably, the document contains no radar data, no corroborating witness accounts, and no follow-up findings. It is a single pilot's observation recorded through the FAA's standard reporting channel. The metadata listing this as a 2023 incident appears to be a cataloguing discrepancy — the source document itself dates the event to February 2021.
