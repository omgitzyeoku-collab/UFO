# FAA Logged Civilian Report of Glowing Sphere Over Texas

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report documenting a civilian caller's sighting of an amber glowing sphere near Deweyville, Texas on the evening of 26 October 2022. The object was described as moving at high speed before stopping mid-air and ascending straight up; the FAA closed the case with no identification or resolution noted.

This document is a SKYWATCH Incident Report filed by the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA), coded as an "Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon." It was logged at 00:45 on 26 October 2022 by FAA headquarters in Denver (HQ DEN) and is now marked as closed.

A civilian caller reported observing, from the ground, an amber glowing sphere in the vicinity of Deweyville, Texas. The caller described the object moving at a high rate of speed from southwest to northeast, then stopping in mid-air and ascending straight up. The event occurred at approximately 9:30 p.m. local time.

The FAA took the report but denied the caller's request for additional information or details about the sighting. The caller was referred to the National UFO Reporting Hotline. The case was closed with no resolution or identification of the object noted in the record.

The report is brief — a single page capturing the basics of a phone call rather than a detailed investigation. There is no mention of radar data, photographs, additional witnesses, or any follow-up inquiry by the FAA.

## What to know

- This is a single civilian witness report with no corroborating radar data, sensor readings, or additional observers mentioned.
- The report is a secondhand account: FAA HQ Denver recorded what the caller told them over the phone, not what an investigator directly observed.
- The case is marked 'Closed' with no resolution — the FAA did not identify or explain the reported object.
- The observation was ground-based and purely visual; no instrumented or technical data supports the description of the object's movement.
- Redaction levels are low, but the document is inherently limited — it captures a phone report, not an investigation.
