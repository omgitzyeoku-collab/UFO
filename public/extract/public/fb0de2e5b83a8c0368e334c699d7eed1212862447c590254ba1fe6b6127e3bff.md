# Two Commercial Flights Reported Unexplained Lights Off California Coast

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents an October 2023 sighting in which an Alaska Airlines crew and a Southwest Airlines crew independently reported unidentified lights travelling along the Northern California coastline above 36,000 feet. The case was closed with no explanation recorded.

On 28 October 2023 at 05:10 UTC, the crew of Alaska Airlines flight ASA1233 — a Boeing 737-900 flying from Los Angeles (LAX) to Seattle (SEA) — reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon while cruising northwest at 36,000 feet. The aircraft was approximately 20 nautical miles west of the Mendocino VOR navigation point, off the Northern California coast. The report was filed through Oakland Air Route Traffic Control Centre (ZOA).

The crew described the phenomenon as lights travelling along the coastline above their altitude. No further detail on the lights' shape, colour, speed, or duration appears in the document.

A second aircraft, Southwest Airlines flight SWA3098 — a Boeing 737 flying from Portland (PDX) to San José (SJC) at 39,000 feet — reported the same phenomenon. Two independent crews observing the same thing from different aircraft strengthens the report, though both accounts flow through the same FAA reporting system.

The FAA classified the incident under its primary code for Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon and logged it as case 237UAP00308. The case status is marked closed. No resolution, follow-up investigation, radar data, sensor readings, or air traffic control communications are included in the document.
