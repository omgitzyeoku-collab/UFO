# FAA Logged a Civilian UFO Sighting Near Baltimore in 2015

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report from 23 October 2015 documenting a civilian phone call about two small, stationary UFOs spotted near Interstate 95 south of Baltimore. The FAA took no investigative action and referred the caller to a UFO reporting centre.

This document is an FAA (Federal Aviation Administration) SKYWATCH incident report — a standardised form the agency uses to log unusual sighting calls routed through its air traffic facilities. It was filed on 23 October 2015 at 23:02 and processed through the Potomac TRACON facility (PCT), under the National Capital Region Coordination Center (NCRCC).

A civilian caller reported seeing two UFOs near exit 50 on Route 95 (Caton Road), south of Baltimore. The caller described the objects as small, flying at low altitude, and stationary. No further details were provided beyond that initial description.

The FAA did not investigate. The facility was advised to direct the caller to a dedicated UFO reporting centre and was given a phone number. The case was immediately marked as closed with the primary code "OTHER." No radar data, sensor readings, or additional witnesses are referenced in the report.

The record's metadata lists an incident date of 2023, but the document itself is dated October 2015. The 2023 date likely reflects when the record was processed or released to the public, not when the sighting occurred. Redaction in this document is minimal.
