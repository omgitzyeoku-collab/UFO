# Commercial Pilot Reported Balloon-Like Object at 37,000 Feet in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA SKYWATCH incident report documents a commercial pilot sighting of a large balloon-like object at high altitude near Billings, Montana in early February 2023. The case was closed with no identification or resolution noted in the document.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report filed under case number 237UAP00147. It records a pilot-reported unidentified aerial phenomenon (UAP) observed during a commercial flight in early February 2023 — a period when US attention to high-altitude balloons was heightened following the Chinese surveillance balloon incidents.

The reporting aircraft, flying under callsign QXE2084 (a Horizon Air commercial flight), was travelling westbound at Flight Level 340 — approximately 34,000 feet — when the crew spotted an object roughly 73 nautical miles from Billings, Montana. They described it as appearing to be a large balloon at an altitude between approximately 37,000 and 39,000 feet, several thousand feet above their own position.

The report notes that an entity or office abbreviated as "JH" was notified of the incident, though the document does not define what JH stands for. The case is marked as closed. No resolution, formal identification, or follow-up findings appear in this document.

It is worth noting that the crew's description of the object as a balloon is their impression, not a confirmed identification. The document contains no mention of corroborating radar data, sensor readings, or independent observations from other aircraft or ground stations.
