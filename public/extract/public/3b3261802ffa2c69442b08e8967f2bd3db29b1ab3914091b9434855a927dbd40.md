# Multiple Pilots Reported Bright Lights Above Alabama in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a sighting on 16 August 2023 in which an American Airlines crew and several other aircraft reported two bright lights circling at very high altitude near Huntsville, Alabama. The case was closed with no explanation provided.

On 16 August 2023 at 06:00 UTC, the crew of American Airlines flight 1397 — a Boeing 737-800 flying from Miami to Kansas City at 34,000 feet — reported seeing what appeared to be two aircraft refueling well above their altitude in the vicinity of Huntsville, Alabama. The crew described two bright lights roughly 60 miles north of Birmingham, circling at very high altitude. The lights would stay on for a few minutes and then disappear.

The sighting was not limited to a single aircraft. Multiple independent sources reported the same phenomenon: another commercial flight (Spirit Airlines flight NKS2866), Birmingham Tower personnel, and pilots operating in Atlanta Center's airspace all described lights matching the same pattern in the same general area.

The FAA logged the event through its SKYWATCH system, which tracks unidentified aerial phenomenon reports from commercial aviation. The agency's internal Airway Operations office (AWO) was notified.

Despite the multiple independent witnesses, the case was marked as closed. The report contains no follow-up investigation, no radar data, no sensor readings, and no explanation for what the lights were. The document does not indicate whether any military or other agency was consulted.

## What to know

- This is a single incident report with no attached investigation or resolution — the FAA closed the case without explanation.
