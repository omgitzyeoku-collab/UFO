This document is an auto-transcribed audio recording from NASA's Mercury-Atlas 9 mission, also known as Faith 7, dated 15 May 1963. It captures communications between the pilot in low Earth orbit and ground control during an orbital sunrise.

The pilot describes a brilliant blue band of light beneath the atmospheric haze layer as the spacecraft approached sunrise. He then reports small, brilliant white particles drifting away from the spacecraft, moving rearward along the flight path. Later, firing the pitch-down thruster produced what the pilot called a shower of 'little fireflies' — luminous particles streaming from the vehicle.

The recording also documents the pilot's attempts to locate a deployed xenon strobe beacon. He initially reported hearing deployment noises but not seeing the beacon. He later observed it flashing at an estimated distance of 10 to 12 miles, describing it as comparable to a second-magnitude star with a reddish-brown colour. Separately, the pilot reported a clear view of the entire state of Florida from orbit and noted a main bus voltage of 24 volts.

The mission description identifies the observed particles and 'fireflies' as mission-related equipment — specifically xenon strobe beacons and debris produced by thruster firings — rather than unexplained phenomena. NASA released this audio as part of its UAP (Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena) public document collection, though the content itself points to conventional explanations.
