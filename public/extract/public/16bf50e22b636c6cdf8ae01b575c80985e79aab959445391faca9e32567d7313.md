# Commercial Pilot Reported Silver UFO at 29,000 Feet Near Raleigh-Durham

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA SKYWATCH incident report documents a commercial airline pilot on flight AWI3763 reporting a silver unidentified flying object passing overhead at 29,000 feet, roughly 13 nautical miles southwest of Raleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU), on 26 December 2020. The case was classified under the code "UAS" (Unmanned Aircraft System) and is now closed, with no evasive action taken and no corroborating radar data included.

On 26 December 2020, at 20:55 local time, the pilot of a CRJ2 regional jet operating as commercial flight AWI3763 reported seeing a silver unidentified flying object pass overhead. The aircraft was flying northeast at 29,000 feet, approximately 13 nautical miles southwest of Raleigh-Durham International Airport (RDU), on a route from Columbia, South Carolina (CAE) to Washington Dulles (IAD).

The pilot did not take evasive action. The FAA's UFO reporting centre was notified by headquarters in Denver (HQ DEN) at 21:45, roughly 50 minutes after the sighting. No further details about the object — its size, shape, speed, or behaviour — appear in the report beyond the word "silver".

The FAA assigned the incident the primary code "UAS" (Unmanned Aircraft System), though this may reflect a default categorisation rather than a confirmed identification. The case status is listed as closed. The document contains no indication of follow-up investigation, radar corroboration, or additional witness accounts.

Notably, the incident itself dates to December 2020, though it was surfaced as part of the Department of War's 2023 PURSUE public release. This suggests it is a historical record included in the broader disclosure effort rather than a newly reported event.
