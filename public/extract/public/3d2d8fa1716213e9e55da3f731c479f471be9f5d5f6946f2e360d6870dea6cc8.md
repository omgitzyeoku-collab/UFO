# Two Aircraft Crews Reported Same Unidentified Light Near Dulles Airspace

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report from April 2021 documenting a UFO sighting by the crews of two separate aircraft — a general aviation plane (N39RC) and a JetBlue flight (JBU1380). Both crews observed a white, rapidly circling light at approximately 45,000 feet near Dulles International Airport airspace; the object disappeared simultaneously from both observers' view. The case was closed with no explanation provided.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report, a standardised form the Federal Aviation Administration uses to log unusual airborne observations. It was filed on 26 April 2021 at 05:45 UTC and assigned to the Denver (DEN) point of contact, with a Mandatory Occurrence Report (MOR) initiated under reference ZDC-M-2021/04/26-0001.

The report records that the pilots-in-command of two aircraft — a general aviation plane with callsign N39RC and JetBlue flight JBU1380 — independently reported seeing the same unidentified object. They described it as a white, rapidly circling light at approximately FL450, or 45,000 feet, visible from the right side of both aircraft in the vicinity of IAD231240, a navigation reference point near Dulles International Airport.

Both crews noted the sighting was short in duration and that the object disappeared at the same time from both their perspectives. The simultaneous observation by two independent aircraft is notable because it reduces the likelihood of a single-observer misidentification.

The report's status is marked as "Closed." No resolution, identification, or follow-up findings are included in the document. The only post-event action noted is that "AWO/JH" were notified, though the document does not identify who these recipients are or what further steps were taken.

## What to know

- The report is based entirely on pilot visual observations — no radar data, instrument readings, or other sensor evidence is referenced.
- FL450 (45,000 feet) is near the operational ceiling of most commercial aircraft, making altitude estimates from below inherently unreliable.
- IAD231240 likely refers to a radial and distance from the Dulles VOR navigational beacon — pinpointing the exact geographic location requires an aeronautical chart lookup.
- The case is marked 'Closed' with no explanation or identification provided, meaning the FAA did not publicly resolve what the object was.
- The notification recipients ('AWO/JH') are not identified in the document, so it is unclear who was informed or whether any follow-up investigation occurred.
