This document is an unclassified memorandum dated 5 June 2026, issued by the Office of the Under Secretary of War. It provides an update on an ongoing case analysis by AARO — the All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office, the Pentagon body responsible for investigating unidentified anomalous phenomena.

The incident took place over two days in October 2023, near a sensitive national security site somewhere in the western United States. The specific location is not named. Six federal law enforcement special agents, organised into three teams of two and positioned at multiple viewing angles, reported observing what they described as luminous orange "mother orbs" that appeared to produce smaller red "orbs." The agents reported that the orange orbs appeared for one to two seconds at a time, each releasing a cluster of two to four red orbs. This pattern repeated multiple times over several hours, beginning at approximately dusk. The red orbs displayed seemingly coordinated horizontal motion and apparent changes in altitude. In one instance, a red orb was reported as remaining stationary above a ridgeline for several hours. All agents described the phenomena as silent.

The witnesses provided consistent accounts using similar language. However, all observations described in the available text are eyewitness testimony — no sensor or instrument data is mentioned.

As of June 2026, AARO has not resolved the case.
