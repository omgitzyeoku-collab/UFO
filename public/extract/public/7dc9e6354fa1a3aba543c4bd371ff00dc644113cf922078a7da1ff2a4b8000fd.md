# FAA Report: Diamond-Shaped Object Flew Under Commercial Jet Near Florida

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA SKYWATCH incident report documents a June 2021 sighting by the crew of a commercial Envoy Air flight, who observed a white, diamond-shaped object pass beneath their aircraft at roughly 19,500 feet over the Atlantic east of Palm Beach. The case was closed with no explanation or identification of the object.

On 12 June 2021, the crew of Envoy Air flight ENY3858 — a commercial Embraer E170 flying from Portland, Maine to Miami — reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon to air traffic control. The aircraft was heading south-southwest at flight level 200 (approximately 20,000 feet) when the crew saw an object pass underneath them roughly 40 nautical miles east-northeast of Palm Beach International Airport, over open water.

The crew described the object as white and diamond-shaped, smaller than their aircraft, and travelling fast on a northbound heading at approximately flight level 195 (around 19,500 feet). That puts the vertical separation at only about 500 feet — close enough to qualify as a near-miss in aviation terms. No specific speed estimate was recorded.

The report was filed through FAA Miami Center (facility code ZMA) and assigned the reference MOR ID ZMA-M-2021/06/12-0006. The Palm Beach Sheriff's office was notified. The FAA marked the case as closed, but the document contains no resolution — the object was never identified.

The report is a single-page incident form. There is no radar data, photographs, or sensor evidence attached. The entire account rests on the crew's visual observation alone.
