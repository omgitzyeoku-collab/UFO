# FAA Report: Pilot Spotted 'Jetpack Individual' Near Burbank Airport

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a pilot sighting of what was described as a 'jetpack individual' flying along Highway 101, about 3 nautical miles east of Burbank Airport on October 20, 2021. A Burbank Police Department helicopter searched the area but found nothing, and the case was closed with no explanation given.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report — a standardised form the Federal Aviation Administration uses to log reports of unidentified aerial phenomena (UAP) in US airspace. It records an event on October 20, 2021, at 23:15 local time, involving flight EJA680 departing Burbank Airport.

The crew reported seeing an unidentified aerial phenomenon while flying southeast at 3,000 feet, approximately 3 nautical miles east of Burbank Airport. They described it as a 'jetpack individual' travelling along Highway 101.

Burbank Police Department dispatched a helicopter to search the area. The helicopter crew reported negative results — they found nothing. The FAA classified the report under its primary code of 'Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon' and marked the case as closed. No cause, resolution, or further explanation is recorded in the document.

This sighting is one of several 'jetpack individual' reports made by pilots in the Los Angeles area airspace between 2020 and 2021. No confirmed public explanation has been established for the series of sightings.
