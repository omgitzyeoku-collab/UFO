# Airline Crew Reports Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon Over Tennessee

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA operations center notification documents a UAP sighting reported by the crew of Envoy Air flight 3914 on January 19, 2024, while flying at 36,000 feet near Jackson, Tennessee. The report contains no description of the object and no evasive action was taken.

On the evening of January 19, 2024, the crew of Envoy Air flight 3914 — an Embraer E170 flying from Lexington, Kentucky to Dallas-Fort Worth — reported seeing an unidentified aerial phenomenon. The aircraft was heading southbound at 36,000 feet, approximately 33 nautical miles north of Jackson, Tennessee, at around 7:07 PM Central time.

The sighting was relayed through Memphis Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) to the FAA's Washington Operations Center, which generated this incident notification under record number 237UAP00597. The crew did not take evasive action, and no law enforcement notification was reported.

Notably, the document is a corrected version of an earlier report — the altitude information was updated by FAA Operations. This means a previous version existed with different altitude details, though the original is not included here.

The document provides no description of what the crew actually saw. There is no information about the object's appearance, size, behaviour, speed, or how long it was visible. There is also no radar data, no corroborating witness accounts, and no follow-up investigation or analysis included. This is strictly an internal FAA notification form, not an investigative report or conclusion.
