# Delta crew reported unidentified object at 23,400 feet over Georgia

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA operations-centre log records that the crew of Delta Air Lines flight 1706, a Boeing 717-200, reported seeing an unidentified anomalous phenomenon while flying at roughly 23,400 feet near Rutledge, Georgia on 14 April 2024. No evasive action was taken and no law-enforcement notification was filed.

This document is a preliminary incident notification from the FAA's Washington Operations Center (WOC). It logs a report relayed from the Eastern Regional Operations Center (E-ROC) about a UAP — unidentified anomalous phenomenon — sighted by a commercial airline crew.

On 14 April 2024 at approximately 7:40 a.m. Eastern time, the crew of Delta Air Lines flight 1706, operating a Boeing 717-200 on the Atlanta-to-Charlotte route, reported observing a UAP at flight level 234 — roughly 23,400 feet. The sighting occurred near an aviation waypoint called PHIIL, in the vicinity of Rutledge, Georgia (coordinates 33.63°N, 83.61°W).

The log notes that no evasive action was taken by the aircraft and that no law-enforcement notification was reported. There is no description of the object's shape, size, movement, or behaviour in the document.

The record is a short operational log entry, not a full investigation report. It contains no radar data, sensor evidence, photographs, or follow-up findings. It simply confirms that the crew made the report and that it was logged through standard FAA channels.
