# FAA Report: Pilot Spotted Unidentified Object Over Virginia in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This is a preliminary FAA incident report from 26 January 2023 documenting a pilot's sighting of an unidentified aerial phenomenon near Lynchburg, Virginia. The crew of a Dassault Falcon 2000 business jet reported observing an object traveling southwest and descending into cloud cover, tracking it for approximately 100 miles.

On 26 January 2023 at 7:15 PM Eastern time, the FAA's Washington Operations Center logged a report of an unidentified aerial phenomenon. The report came from the crew of a Dassault Falcon 2000 business jet (registration N541Z) flying from Teterboro, New Jersey to Charlotte, North Carolina.

The aircraft was descending from Flight Level 430 — roughly 43,000 feet — when the crew observed an object traveling southwest and descending into cloud cover. The sighting occurred approximately 10 nautical miles north of Lynchburg, Virginia, at coordinates 37.47°N, 79.20°W. According to the report, the crew tracked the object for 100 miles.

The document is marked as preliminary information from FAA Operations and carries the reference number WOC 7-3333. It does not include any conclusions about what the object was, nor does it reference corroborating evidence such as radar data or observations from other aircraft. The initials HM/JW appear on the report but no full names are provided.

There is minimal redaction in this document. What limits it is not censorship but scope — this is a single-page preliminary report, not a full investigation. The FAA logged the sighting but the document contains no follow-up findings or final determination.
