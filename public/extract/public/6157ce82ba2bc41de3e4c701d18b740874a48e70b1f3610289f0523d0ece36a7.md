# FAA Report: Unknown Object Shot Past Airliner Near Fort Lauderdale

**FAA · 2023**

> This is an FAA incident report from August 2021 documenting a commercial flight crew's sighting of an unidentified aerial phenomenon that appeared to shoot straight up from the ground past their aircraft near Fort Lauderdale. The case was closed with no explanation or resolution noted.

On 30 August 2021, the crew of a commercial Embraer E175 aircraft (callsign ASH6208) reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon while departing Miami International Airport (MIA) en route to Washington Dulles (IAD). The aircraft was climbing through 11,500 feet, approximately 15 nautical miles north of Fort Lauderdale-Hollywood International Airport (FLL), when the crew spotted something off the left side of the plane.

According to the report, the unknown phenomenon "shot straight up from the ground past the plane." No further description of the object — its size, shape, colour, or how long it was visible — was provided by the crew. The report notes simply: "No other information provided."

The FAA logged this under its SKYWATCH programme and classified it with the primary code "Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon." The case status is marked as closed, but the document contains no resolution, no follow-up investigation findings, and no attempted explanation for what the crew observed.

The reporting facility was MIA, and the operator is listed as ASH, a commercial carrier. The aircraft tail number field was left blank, which limits the ability to independently verify the specific aircraft involved.
