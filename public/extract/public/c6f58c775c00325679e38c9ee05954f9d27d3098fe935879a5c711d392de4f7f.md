# Two Pilots Over Alaska Reported Same Unidentified Bright Light

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA incident report documents a UPS cargo pilot sighting of an unexplained bright white light above their aircraft over Alaska in April 2023, corroborated by a second aircraft on a similar route. The FAA assessed it as consistent with a Starlink satellite train and closed the case.

On 10 April 2023 at 11:08 UTC, a UPS commercial flight cruising at 35,000 feet reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon while flying northeast-bound over Alaska, roughly 148 nautical miles northwest of Sparrevohn. The crew described a bright white light at an unknown altitude above them, visible off the aircraft's left side. The flight was en route from Incheon, South Korea to Anchorage.

The sighting was not isolated. A second aircraft — callsign AJT1020, a Boeing 767 also flying from Incheon to Anchorage at 33,000 feet — independently reported the same light.

The FAA's assessment concluded the sighting was consistent with a train of Starlink satellites travelling over Alaska. The original document spells it "Skylink," which appears to be a typo. The case was closed with no further investigation referenced.

No sensor data, radar tracks, or imagery are attached to this report. It is a single-page incident record from the FAA's SKYWATCH reporting system, not a detailed analytical product.
