# Alaska Airlines crew reported dark object near Seattle in 2023

**FAA · 2023**

> This FAA preliminary incident report documents a February 2023 sighting by the crew of Alaska Airlines flight 688, who observed a dark object at their altitude while departing Seattle. The object was tentatively described as a possible drone or balloon, but no confirmed identification was made.

On 16 February 2023, the crew of Alaska Airlines flight 688 — a Boeing 737-900 flying from Seattle to Las Vegas — reported an unidentified object to Seattle Air Route Traffic Control Centre (ARTCC). The aircraft was approximately 18 nautical miles south-southeast of Seattle-Tacoma International Airport, heading south-southeast at 10,700 feet.

The crew described the object as dark, positioned at their 2 o'clock (roughly front-right), and at the same altitude as the aircraft. They tentatively characterised it as a possible drone or balloon, though this was an initial impression rather than a confirmed identification.

The flight crew took no evasive action, and no law enforcement notification was reported. The FAA's Washington Operations Centre logged the event as a preliminary report — meaning this document records the initial facts as relayed by air traffic control, not the outcome of any deeper investigation.

The coordinates in the report (47.2°N, 122.2°W) place the sighting near the Tacoma/Federal Way area of Washington state, consistent with the described position relative to Seattle.

## What to know
