Support UAP Files

UAP Files is free for everyone. No paywalls, no ads, no signups, no tracking, no accounts. It will stay that way — that is not a promise we can afford to break, because being trustworthy is the only thing this archive has.

Sponsor the project

Recurring support through GitHub Sponsors. It funds the running costs and nothing else — there is no tier, no perk, no early access, no supporter-only content. Everything on this site is free to everyone at the same time, and always will be.

♡ Sponsor on GitHub or star the repo — free, and it genuinely helps people find this.

What the money actually pays for

LineWhat it is
ComputeThe verification pipeline reads every document and checks every claim against an exact quote in the source. This is the largest cost by a distance, and it is the whole point of the project.
Transcription + OCRWhisper for audio and video; OCR for 1940s typewriter scans that no machine reads cleanly.
Hosting + bandwidthThe site, and mirroring the binary corpus so the files survive independently of any government website.
DomainModest, annual, unglamorous.

Money we will not take

This matters more than the money. We will not accept funding from:

  • Governments, defence contractors, or intelligence-adjacent organisations
  • Media outlets with active UAP coverage
  • Anyone seeking influence over what we publish, what we summarise, or how we tier it
  • Anyone expecting the archive to support a conclusion — in either direction

The documents say what they say. Most of them are mundane. A record marked "unresolved" is not a record saying "aliens", and no amount of money will make us write it as one. Every donation must be refusable, and any that isn't will be refused.

What we will never do

  • No ads. Not now, not at scale.
  • No paywall. Not for documents, summaries, search, or bulk download.
  • No supporter-only tier. A perk tier is a paywall wearing a hat.
  • No selling your data. We hold nothing to sell — see the privacy policy.

Ways to help that cost nothing

  • Report an error. Every document has a "report an error" link. Corrections are published in a public, append-only log — we don't silently edit. A good correction is worth more to this archive than a donation.
  • Use it and cite it. Our summaries are CC-BY 4.0; the government records are public domain. Take them.
  • Tell someone who needs it — a journalist, a researcher, a sceptic.

Transparency

The code is open source and the pipeline is auditable end to end. If this project ever takes meaningful funding, the source and the amount will be published on this page.
