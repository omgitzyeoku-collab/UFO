Copa Airlines Crew Reports Flashing White Light Over Florida Keys

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

This FAA incident notification records a UAP sighting by a Copa Airlines Boeing 737 crew flying at 37,000 feet near the Florida Keys on 10 June 2023. The pilots described a stationary white light that switched on and off periodically, and multiple other aircraft in the area reported seeing the same thing.

On 10 June 2023 at 02:26 Eastern time, the crew of Copa Airlines flight 404 — a Panama-registered Boeing 737-800 flying from Panama City to Washington Dulles — reported an unidentified aerial phenomenon to Miami Air Route Traffic Control Centre (ARTCC). The aircraft was heading north at 37,000 feet, roughly 3.1 nautical miles southwest of Florida Keys Marathon International Airport. The crew described the object as a stationary white light directly ahead of them (the "12 o'clock position") that turned on and off periodically. They did not take evasive action, and the sighting had no reported impact on flight operations. Notably, the report states that multiple other aircraft in the vicinity also reported similar sightings, though none of those aircraft are identified by name or flight number in the document. This record is a preliminary notification from the FAA's Washington Operations Center — it documents the initial report, not the outcome of any investigation. No radar data, sensor recordings, or photographs are referenced. The description relies entirely on what the pilots saw with their eyes. The document shows minimal redaction. The coordinates place the sighting at approximately 24.69°N, 81.09°W, over the waters near Marathon, Florida.

What to know

  • This is a preliminary incident notification, not a completed investigation — no conclusions or follow-up findings are included.
  • The description is based solely on pilot visual observation; no radar data, sensor recordings, or photographs are referenced.
  • The other aircraft that reportedly saw the same phenomenon are not identified, so independent corroboration cannot be verified from this document alone.
  • A stationary flashing white light has many possible explanations (satellites, high-altitude balloons, celestial objects, other aircraft); this report does not attempt to identify the source.
  • Redaction levels are low — the document appears largely complete as released.
