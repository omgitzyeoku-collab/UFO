Delta crew reported multiple orbiting lights over Alabama in 2024

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

This FAA preliminary report documents a Delta Air Lines crew sighting multiple lights orbiting at various altitudes ahead of their aircraft near Huntsville, Alabama, on 14 February 2024. The lights were not detected on radar, and no evasive action was taken. No follow-up investigation results are included.

This document is a preliminary information report from the FAA's Washington Operations Center — the agency's round-the-clock facility that logs aviation incidents nationwide. It records a UAP (Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon) sighting reported by a commercial airline flight crew. On 14 February 2024 at approximately 3:30 a.m. Central time, the crew of Delta flight 757 — a Boeing 767-400 flying from Phoenix to Atlanta — observed multiple lights ahead of the aircraft. The lights appeared to be orbiting at various altitudes. The aircraft was cruising at 37,000 feet, roughly 50 nautical miles west of Huntsville, Alabama. The crew did not take evasive action, indicating the lights were not perceived as an immediate collision threat. Notably, the UAP was not observed by radar, meaning only the flight crew's visual report exists as evidence. The FAA filed this as a preliminary report. The document contains no conclusions about what the lights were, no follow-up investigation results, and no additional corroborating observations from other aircraft or ground stations. It is a record of a single crew's report, logged through standard FAA channels.

What to know

  • This is a preliminary report only — not a completed investigation or any kind of official conclusion about what was observed.
  • The sighting is single-source: one flight crew's visual observation with no radar corroboration and no reports from other aircraft or ground observers.
  • DAL757 is a radio call sign, not the aircraft type — the plane was a Boeing 767-400, not a Boeing 757.
  • No follow-up findings, analysis, or resolution are included in this document.
  • The incident location is approximate, based on the crew's reported position relative to Huntsville, Alabama.
