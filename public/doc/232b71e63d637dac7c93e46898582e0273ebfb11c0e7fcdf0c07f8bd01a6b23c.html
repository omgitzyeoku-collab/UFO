This record is Part I of a preliminary crew debriefing from NASA's Gemini 4 mission, dated June 16, 1965. The incident location is listed as the North Atlantic Ocean. The document was originally classified, with unauthorized disclosure prohibited by law, and fell under Group 4 downgrade rules requiring declassification at three-year intervals. On November 20, 1973, an official identified as Z. Zeman changed the document's status to unclassified. That declassification stamp is the clearest information available on the cover page. The document is an image file, and the text extracted by optical character recognition (OCR) is heavily garbled. What survives in readable form is limited to the classification cover page — the substantive content of the crew debriefing itself is not present in the extracted text. Because the actual debriefing transcript is unreadable in this release, nothing can be confirmed about what the crew reported during the mission. This is Part I only; additional parts of the debriefing may exist and could contain further details.

What to know

  • Only the classification cover page is readable — the actual crew debriefing content was not successfully extracted from this image file.
  • The document shows heavy redaction evidence, meaning significant portions may have been obscured before release.
  • OCR quality is poor, with many garbled characters, so even the cover page text is partially reconstructed.
