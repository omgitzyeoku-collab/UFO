This record is a single cover page from Part II of the Gemini 5 flight crew technical debriefing. Gemini 5 flew from August 30 to September 2, 1965, launching from Cape Kennedy, Florida. The debriefing transcript was originally stamped CONFIDENTIAL and was later declassified, with a declassification date of November 20 (the year is unreadable due to poor scan quality). The declassification appears to have been carried out under the authority of Executive Order 11652, though the OCR rendering of that reference is garbled. Because this is an image-type document — a scanned photograph of the original page — very little usable text was captured during extraction. What survives is limited to classification markings and the declassification stamp. The substantive content of the debriefing, which according to the archive's metadata covers pages 157 through 220 and includes crew reports of visual sightings of debris, 'snow,' and 'glittering pieces,' is not present in this extracted material. No conclusions, analysis, or agency assessments appear on this page. It functions solely as an administrative cover sheet recording the document's classification history.

What to know
