FBI Used Forensic Sketch Artist to Draw a UAP Sighting

FBI 2022 Colorado Springs, Colorado, U.S. ✓ verified against source

This FBI document is a forensic sketch interview form (FD-1057) recording an interview conducted to produce a visual reconstruction of an unidentified aerial phenomenon (UAP) reported near Colorado Springs, Colorado. Nearly all substantive details — the witness's identity, the description of the object, and key dates — are redacted, leaving only the procedural framework visible.

This document is an FBI form known as an FD-1057 — the same type of forensic sketch interview form the Bureau uses when a witness sits down with a sketch artist to reconstruct what they saw. In this case, the subject was not a criminal suspect but an unidentified aerial phenomenon, or UAP. The interview was conducted by a forensic artist from the FBI's Operational Projects Unit (OPU). A witness provided information to help produce a visual recreation of the UAP. A sketch draft was produced and enclosed with the document, though the sketch itself is not available in the extracted text — only the interview form survives. The incident is catalogued as occurring near Colorado Springs, Colorado, in 2022, but the document header carries a date of July 2020. The interview itself is noted as taking place on 7/24, with the year redacted. The relationship between these dates is unclear. The level of redaction is extreme. Nearly all identifying details — the witness's name, the interviewing agents, specific locations, and crucially, the actual description of what the witness saw — have been blacked out. No substantive account of the UAP survives. The document confirms that the FBI took the report seriously enough to deploy a forensic sketch process, but reveals almost nothing about the sighting itself. The FBI reached no stated conclusion in the visible portions of this document. The report remains categorised as unresolved.

What to know

  • Extreme redaction: the actual witness description of the UAP is entirely obscured, so this document tells us the FBI conducted an interview but not what was reported.
  • This is a single-source witness account with no corroboration documented in the visible portions.
  • There is a date discrepancy: the header reads July 2020 but the metadata lists the incident year as 2022 — the reason for this mismatch is not explained.
  • The forensic sketch itself is not included in the extracted text, so the visual reconstruction of the UAP cannot be reviewed here.
  • Poor OCR quality (the document is an image) means some text fragments in the original may be unreliable.
