American Airlines Crew Reported Spinning Lights Over Nebraska in 2023

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

This FAA incident report documents a sighting by the crew of American Airlines flight AAL1308 on 3 December 2023. While flying at approximately 32,000 feet over Nebraska, the crew observed spinning flashing lights above their altitude that did not appear on air traffic control radar.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report — part of the agency's system for logging unidentified aerial phenomena reported by pilots and air traffic controllers. It covers an event on 3 December 2023 at 03:15, involving American Airlines flight AAL1308, an Airbus A321 travelling from Philadelphia to San Francisco. While flying westbound at Flight Level 320 (approximately 32,000 feet), the crew reported seeing spinning flashing lights above their altitude. The sighting occurred 31 nautical miles south-southwest of O'Neill, Nebraska. The report was filed through Denver Air Route Traffic Control Centre (ZDV). Air traffic control radar did not detect the object. The Aviation Weather Office was notified. The case was assigned the identifier 237UAP00327 and the internal reference ZDV-M-2023/12/02-0004. The FAA marked the case as closed. No resolution or explanation for the sighting is provided in the document.

What to know

  • This is a single-source pilot report with no corroborating radar data or independent witness accounts.
  • The document provides no detailed description of the object's size, shape, speed, or how long the crew observed it.
  • The case is marked closed with no explanation or conclusion offered by the FAA.
  • No redactions are evident in the document, but the level of detail recorded is minimal.
  • A visual sighting without radar confirmation does not establish what the object was — it confirms only that the crew reported seeing something.
