FAA Report: Pilot Spotted Apparent Jet Pack Figure Near Oakland

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

This FAA incident report documents a general aviation pilot who reported seeing what appeared to be a person in a jet pack at 3,000 feet altitude, about 6 nautical miles east of Oakland, California, on 29 August 2021. The case was classified as an Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon and closed without resolution.

On 29 August 2021 at 20:35, a pilot flying a light aircraft (an EV97, callsign N712MF) on a visual flight rules trip to Reid-Hillview Airport reported an unusual sighting to the FAA. While heading southeast at 3,500 feet, approximately 6 nautical miles east of Oakland International Airport, the pilot observed what appeared to be a person in a jet pack at roughly 3,000 feet — 500 feet below the aircraft. The pilot took no evasive action. The FAA logged the incident under its SKYWATCH system and assigned it the primary code of Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon. The report is brief and purely descriptive: it records what the pilot said they saw, with no corroborating radar data or additional witness statements included. The case is marked as closed. No identification of the object was provided, and no further investigation or resolution is noted in the document. The FAA did not confirm or deny whether the object was actually a person with a jet pack. This report is part of a broader pattern of similar "jet pack" sightings reported by pilots in the Los Angeles and San Francisco Bay areas during 2020 and 2021, several of which drew media attention at the time. Whether these sightings share a common explanation remains unresolved in the public record.

What to know

  • This is a single-source pilot observation. No radar data, photographs, or additional witnesses are referenced in the report.
  • The pilot described the object as a 'person in a jet pack,' but the FAA did not confirm that identification. The description is the pilot's interpretation of what they saw.
  • The case was closed without any stated resolution or explanation of what the object was.
  • Redaction levels in this document are low — the report appears largely complete, but it is also very brief, containing minimal investigative detail.
  • Similar jet pack sightings were reported near Los Angeles and the San Francisco Bay Area in 2020–2021; this report should be read in that wider context, though no formal connection between cases is established here.
