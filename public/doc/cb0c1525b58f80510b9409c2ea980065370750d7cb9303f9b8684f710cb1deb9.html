On 7 December 1972, during the Apollo 17 mission's transit from Earth to the Moon (known as cislunar space), the crew reported seeing unusual objects outside their spacecraft. This audio excerpt, catalogued as NASA-UAP-D009, captures their real-time observations and was released as part of the PURSUE public collection. Most of the fragments seemed to be twinkling and slowly drifting away from the spacecraft, with no apparent motion between the individual pieces. The crew's own leading explanation was mundane debris — possibly material shed from the Saturn V rocket's S-IVB upper stage, which had been part of the launch vehicle. As one crew member put it regarding the S-IVB theory, "it's a wild guess." They attempted to photograph the fragments, noting they would shoot at frame 70.
