Frontier Airlines crew reported airliner-sized object hovering near Tampa

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

An FAA operations center log from 19 November 2023 records the crew of Frontier Airlines flight 2402 reporting a large unidentified aerial phenomenon — described as the size of an airliner — hovering at roughly 25,000 feet, about 40 nautical miles northwest of Tampa, Florida. No evasive action was taken and no law enforcement was notified.

This document is an incident log from the FAA's Washington Operations Center, initially relayed by the Eastern Regional Operations Center (E-ROC). It records a pilot sighting reported on 19 November 2023 at 5:40 PM Eastern time. The crew of Frontier Airlines flight 2402 — an Airbus A320neo flying from Dallas/Fort Worth to Tampa — reported observing a large unidentified aerial phenomenon at flight level 250 (approximately 25,000 feet). The crew described the object as the "size of an airliner" and said it was hovering roughly 40 nautical miles northwest of Tampa, over the Gulf of Mexico off Florida's west coast. The coordinates logged in the report place the sighting at approximately 28.4°N, 83.0°W. The crew did not take evasive action, and no law enforcement notification was reported. The document is a preliminary operations center log entry — not a full investigation or final conclusion. The FAA did not offer an explanation for what the crew observed. There is minimal redaction in this record. However, the report is notable for what it lacks: no radar data, no corroborating witness accounts from other aircraft, and no follow-up investigation are included in this document.

What to know

  • This is a single-source pilot report. No corroborating radar data or additional witness accounts are included in the document.
  • The description of the object as 'hovering' is the crew's characterisation and was not independently verified by radar or other sensors in this record.
  • This is a preliminary operations center log entry, not a completed investigation with findings or conclusions.
  • The coordinates place the sighting over the Gulf of Mexico, off Florida's west coast — not over land near Tampa itself.
  • No explanation or identification of the object was provided by the FAA in this document.
