FBI October, 2024 Northeastern United States ✓ verified against source

This document is an FBI FD-1057, a standard investigative activity form used to record agent contacts and evidence collection. It shows that in October 2024, FBI special agents reached out to a U.S. person in the northeastern United States who had footage of unidentified objects near their residence. The individual provided multiple video clips showing what the form describes as objects "appearing to be unknown UAS or UAP" — meaning unidentified drones or aerial phenomena. The person had previously been referred to the FBI by a liaison contact, though the identity of that contact is redacted. The FBI does not draw any conclusion in this document about what the objects are. The language is cautious: the objects only "appear to be" unknown.
