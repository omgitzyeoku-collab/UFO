FBI Document Records Intelligence Official's Account of Multiple Orb Encounters

FBI Late 2025 United States ✓ verified against source

A SECRET//NOFORN FBI interview summary describes a senior US intelligence official's first-hand account of encountering numerous unidentified orbs during a helicopter search mission at a US military facility in late 2025. The objects displayed behaviors including splitting apart, forming patterns, swarming, and outpacing a pursuing helicopter — but the document is so heavily redacted that names, dates, locations, and facility details are all blacked out.

This document is an FBI 302 — a standard form agents use to summarize witness interviews — classified SECRET//NOFORN (secret, not releasable to foreign nationals). It records the statement of a senior US intelligence official identified only as "WITNESS 1," describing events at an unnamed US military facility on a redacted date in 2025. The encounter began around 5:00 PM when WITNESS 1 and a helicopter crew launched a daytime search of a nearby mountain range. The mission was prompted by earlier reports from personnel who had seen orbs or lights and heard unexplained thuds "as if something had fallen and hit the ground." After dark, using Forward Looking Infrared (FLIR) sensors and Night Vision Goggles (NVG), a ground observation team detected what they described as a "super-hot" orb hovering at ground level. The orb then traveled east and south at high speed, split into two objects, and covered roughly 20 miles — fast enough that the helicopter broke off pursuit because it could not keep up. The ground team reported the orb came within ten feet of the helicopter. A co-pilot then observed, through NVG, a third object emerge from the two and fly off in a different direction. Over the next hour, the witnesses reported repeated sightings: formations of four to six orbs flaring up one at a time in horizontal lines, then extinguishing in reverse order over 10 to 15 seconds. At 10:18 PM, they observed a "swarm of lights too many to count" moving in all directions. At 10:49 PM, three distinct orbs appeared in a triangle formation within a larger swarm. The orbs were described as oval-shaped, orange with a white or yellow center, emitting light in all directions. At 10:57 PM, four orbs appeared directly over military aircraft as they descended to land — the witness stated the orbs appeared to break off from the helicopter and pursue those aircraft. Five military aircraft were confirmed to be conducting a training exercise in the same airspace. The helicopter returned to base around 11:16 PM. The document does not contain any agency conclusion about what the orbs were. It is a record of one person's account. Nearly every identifying detail — names, specific dates, facility names, coordinates, organizational affiliations, and call signs — has been redacted, making independent verification of the events impossible.

What to know

  • This is a single witness's account as summarized by an FBI agent — FBI 302s are not verbatim transcripts, and no independent corroboration of these specific events is provided in the document.
  • The redaction is extreme: all names, exact dates, locations, coordinates, facility identifiers, and organizational details are blacked out, so there is no way to independently verify where or exactly when this happened.
  • Some of the orb sightings were only visible through Night Vision Goggles and could not be seen with the naked eye, meaning the observations depend on the equipment and its interpretation.
  • The helicopter's FLIR camera reportedly could not capture many of the sightings because the orbs were above the aircraft and outside the camera's field of view, limiting potential recorded evidence.
  • Five military aircraft were conducting a training exercise in the same airspace during the encounter, and an unspecified system test had been conducted at the facility earlier that day — the relevance of both is unknown due to redaction.
