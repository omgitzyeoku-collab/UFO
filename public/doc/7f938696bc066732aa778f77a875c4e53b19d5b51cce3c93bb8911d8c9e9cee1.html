Frontier Airlines Crew Reports Flashing Lights at 35,000 Feet Over North Carolina

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

An FAA operations center report documents the crew of Frontier Airlines flight 1164 observing two intermittent flashing lights off the left side of their aircraft while flying at 35,000 feet near Southern Pines, North Carolina on April 17, 2023. The lights were traveling in the opposite direction at the same altitude, and no identification was made.

This document is an FAA Washington Operations Center incident report filed on April 17, 2023. It records a pilot observation of an unidentified aerial phenomenon — meaning the crew saw something they could not identify. The crew of Frontier Airlines flight 1164, an Airbus A321 flying from Orlando to Philadelphia, reported seeing two intermittent flashing lights off the left side of their aircraft. The sighting occurred while the plane was flying northeast at 35,000 feet, approximately 14 nautical miles south of the Sandhills navigational point (known as SDZ) near Southern Pines, North Carolina. The lights were traveling southeast — the opposite direction — at the same altitude. Washington Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC), the controlling air traffic facility, was advised of the report. No law enforcement agency was notified. The FAA report does not reach a conclusion about what the lights were. This is a preliminary operations center log, not a completed investigation. No radar data, photographs, or additional witness accounts are included in the document.

What to know

  • This is a single-source pilot report. No corroborating radar data or additional witness accounts are included in the document.
  • The description of two intermittent flashing lights is consistent with conventional aircraft anti-collision lighting, though no identification was made.
  • This is a preliminary FAA operations center report, not a completed investigation or formal finding.
  • The document shows minimal redaction, but also contains minimal detail — it is a brief operational log, not an in-depth case file.
  • The coordinates place the sighting near Southern Pines, NC, but the exact location carries moderate confidence due to how the position was recorded.
