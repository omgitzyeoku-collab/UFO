NASA Routing Slip from 1966 Gemini 9 Debriefing Is Nearly Illegible

NASA June 3-6, 1966 Low Earth Orbit ✓ verified against source

This document is a NASA internal routing slip (Form 26) associated with the Gemini 9 mission debriefing from June 1966. The image quality is so degraded that almost no substantive content is readable — the multi-page debriefing transcript referenced in the collection description is not present in this extraction.

This record, catalogued as NASA-UAP-D022, is a photograph of a NASA routing slip — a standard internal form (Form 26, revised January 1963) used to circulate documents between offices and personnel within the agency. It is associated with the Gemini 9 mission, which took place from 3 to 6 June 1966 in low Earth orbit. The routing slip itself is a cover page. It contains checkboxes for directing an attached document through various NASA offices and a remarks section. However, the image quality is extremely poor, and the vast majority of the text is garbled and illegible. The collection metadata describes the attached debriefing as containing astronaut accounts of "flashing lights" and "sparkles" observed during the mission, reportedly discussed on pages 2 through 5. None of that content is present in this extraction, which covers only the single-page routing slip. Because the substantive debriefing transcript is missing from the available material, no claims about what the astronauts reported can be verified from this source alone. What can be confirmed is limited to two facts: this is an official NASA routing slip, and it uses Form 26 (revised January 1963). The document is classified as having "extreme" evidence of redaction or degradation, and the OCR extraction — the process of converting a photographed page into readable text — produced almost nothing usable.

What to know

  • This extraction contains only the routing slip cover page, not the multi-page debriefing transcript described in the collection metadata.
  • The image quality is so degraded that OCR produced almost entirely garbled text — substantive analysis from this source alone is impossible.
  • Claims about astronaut observations of flashing lights or sparkles cannot be verified from the available extracted text.
  • This is a photograph of a document (IMG type), which typically yields far worse text extraction than a native digital PDF.
  • Only two facts could be confirmed with high confidence: that it is a NASA routing slip, and the form number (Form 26, Rev Jan 1963).
