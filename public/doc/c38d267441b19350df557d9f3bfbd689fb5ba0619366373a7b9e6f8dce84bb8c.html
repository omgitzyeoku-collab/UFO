This document is an FBI FD-302 form, the standard template the Bureau uses to summarize witness interviews. It records a US citizen's first-hand account of encountering an unidentified anomalous phenomenon (UAP) at a US test site in September 2023. The specific day within that month is redacted. What survives on this page is a single descriptive detail: the witness initially believed the light they observed was a meteor coming straight toward them and burning up in the atmosphere. The document is extremely redacted throughout. The witness's name, the precise date, and the specific location are all blacked out. No agency conclusion or follow-up assessment is visible in the available page. There is no indication in the surviving text of what the FBI ultimately determined the object to be, or whether any further investigation was conducted. Because this is a single witness's subjective recollection as summarized by an FBI interviewer, and the majority of the interview content is either on the missing first page or hidden under redactions, the document on its own provides very limited information about the incident.
