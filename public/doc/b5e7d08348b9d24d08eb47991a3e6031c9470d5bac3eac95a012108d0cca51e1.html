Airline Crew Reports Multiple Lights Circling at 37,000 Feet Over Wisconsin

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

An FAA incident notification records that the crew of a Boeing 767-300 cargo flight from Sacramento to Chicago-Rockford saw multiple lights moving and circling at or above 37,000 feet near Mauston, Wisconsin, on February 19, 2024. Air traffic control radar did not detect the objects.

This document is an FAA Washington Operations Center incident notification — a preliminary report filed when something unusual is reported in US airspace. It is not a full investigation or a final conclusion. It records a single sighting by one flight crew. On February 19, 2024, at 4:50 AM Central time, the crew of Air Transport 3498 — a Boeing 767-300 flying from Sacramento (SMF) to Chicago-Rockford (RFD) — reported seeing multiple lights moving and circling at or above flight level 370, roughly 37,000 feet. The pilot first observed the lights approximately 20 nautical miles east-northeast of the Mauston VOR navigation point in Wisconsin. Salt Lake City Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) relayed the report. Notably, the objects were not detected on air traffic control radar. The document provides no description of the lights beyond their movement pattern — no shape, size, colour, or speed is recorded. The FAA filed this as a UAP (unidentified anomalous phenomena) notification. There is no indication in the document of any follow-up investigation, explanation, or conclusion about what the crew observed.

What to know

  • This is a single-source report from one flight crew. No other aircraft or ground observers are recorded as corroborating the sighting.
  • The objects did not appear on ATC radar, which means there is no independent sensor data backing the visual observation.
  • The document is a preliminary notification, not a completed investigation. No cause or explanation is offered.
  • Very limited detail is provided about the lights — only that they were 'moving and circling.' No shape, size, or behaviour beyond that is described.
  • The coordinates in the document metadata appear to refer to the aircraft's destination (Rockford, IL), not the sighting location (near Mauston, WI). Readers should not confuse the two.
