Digitally Altered Video of Luminous Object Near Kazakhstan Airport Released

Department of War 2022 Kazakhstan ± partially verified

A 17-second cellphone video allegedly showing a luminous phenomenon with trails near Karaganda International Airport in Kazakhstan was uploaded to a classified U.S. network in April 2023. The All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) determined the video was digitally altered before upload, and it lacks a substantiated chain of custody.

This record, designated DOW-UAP-PR072, is a 17-second video that AARO assessed was likely captured on a commercial cellphone's rear-facing camera in March 2022 near Karaganda International Airport, Kazakhstan. The footage shows a luminous phenomenon with trails — though the full description of what was observed appears truncated in the available metadata. An anonymous user uploaded the video to a classified U.S. government network in April 2023, over a year after the alleged capture. AARO explicitly states that the video was digitally altered before it was uploaded, and presents the material as received — meaning no further modifications were made after it entered the classified system. The video surfaced publicly as part of a broader release. On 6 March 2026, eight members of the U.S. House of Representatives requested access to 51 potentially UAP-related records held by the Department of War and the Intelligence Community. AARO identified this video among a collection of responsive materials. AARO's own assessment flags a significant credibility issue: many of the responsive materials, including this one, lack a substantiated chain of custody. The first three seconds of the video contain no content. No corroborating sensor data or additional witnesses are mentioned in the available record.

What to know
