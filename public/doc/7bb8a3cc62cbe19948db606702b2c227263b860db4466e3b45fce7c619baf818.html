Military Infrared Video Shows Unidentified Object Over CENTCOM Region

Department of War 2022 CENTCOM ± partially verified

This 21-second infrared video, catalogued as DOW-UAP-PR053, was likely captured by a U.S. military sensor in the Central Command area of responsibility in October 2022. The video was digitally altered before being uploaded to a classified network in June 2024, and it shows an unidentified object — described by the uploader as cigar-shaped or fast spherical — moving across the sensor's field of view.

In October 2022, a U.S. military platform operating in the CENTCOM (United States Central Command) area of responsibility recorded a 21-second infrared video clip. The footage shows an area of contrast — an object distinguishable from its background — transiting the sensor's field of view from left to right over roughly three seconds. The uploader described it as a "cigar-shaped or fast spherical" unidentified aerial phenomenon (UAP), though that description reflects the uploader's own characterisation, not an official assessment. The video was uploaded to a classified network in June 2024 — nearly two years after the incident. Notably, the Department of War's All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) states the video was digitally altered prior to upload. The nature and extent of that alteration are not specified. AARO presents the footage "as received" and uses hedging language, assessing that the video "likely" came from an infrared military sensor rather than stating it with certainty. This clip is one of 51 potentially UAP-related records that eight members of the U.S. House of Representatives requested access to on 6 March 2026. The records were allegedly held by the Department of War and the Intelligence Community. AARO notes that many of the responsive materials lack a substantiated chain of custody, meaning their authenticity cannot be independently verified. The video's metadata appears truncated, suggesting incomplete information or possible redaction. No additional context — such as the specific platform, crew observations, or follow-up investigation results — is provided in the released material.
