Heavily Redacted 2020 Military Report Documents One Probable UAP in the Arabian Gulf

Department of War Pacific Ocean ✓ verified against source

A U.S. Department of War mission report from 2020 records a single probable Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena (UAP) sighting in the Arabian Gulf region. Six of the document's seven pages are entirely blacked out, and the one partially visible page reveals only that one object was observed, it had no effect on the military mission, and the operator carried on with their original task.

This document is a U.S. military Mission Report — a standard formatted account filed after an operational observation. It was produced by the Department of War and covers an incident in the Arabian Gulf area in 2020. The report is catalogued under the reference "DOW-UAP-D6" with an associated report number AT 12467. The substance of the report is sparse because of extreme redaction. Six of the seven pages are completely blacked out under national-security exemptions. The one partially legible page states that a military operator "observed 1x probable UAP" near grid coordinate 3SKT4255899519, a location consistent with the Arabian Gulf or Middle East region. The observer's identity and military unit are redacted. The report notes that the sighting had "no mission impact" and that the operator "continued original tasking" — meaning whatever they were doing before the sighting, they kept doing it. No description of the object's shape, size, speed, altitude, or behaviour survives the redactions. One notable inconsistency exists in the document's own metadata: the title references the Arabian Gulf, but the cataloguing metadata lists the incident location as the Pacific Ocean. The verified facts flag this as a likely cataloguing error, since the grid coordinate points to the Middle East, not the Pacific. No sensor data, corroborating witnesses, or follow-up analysis is visible in the released text. The document amounts to a single operator's report that something unidentified was seen, with almost everything else removed.

What to know

  • Six of seven pages are entirely redacted — the vast majority of this document's content is hidden from public view, making it impossible to assess what was actually observed.
  • This is a single-source observation: one operator reported seeing one object. No corroborating sensor data, radar tracks, or additional witness accounts are visible in the released material.
  • The document itself notes that the description reflects the reporter's subjective interpretation at the time of the sighting.
  • There is a metadata inconsistency — the title says Arabian Gulf while the cataloguing data says Pacific Ocean. The grid coordinate supports the Arabian Gulf location, suggesting the Pacific Ocean entry is a filing error.
  • Nothing in the visible text identifies what the object was. 'Probable UAP' means the observer believed it was unidentified — it does not imply any particular explanation.
