Commercial Airline Crew Reports Three Unexplained Lights Over Iowa

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

An FAA preliminary report documents a UAP sighting by the crew of a Virgin Atlantic Boeing 787 flying from Las Vegas to London on 10 March 2023. The crew observed three lights of varying brightness near Fort Dodge, Iowa, at 37,000 feet, and multiple other aircraft reported similar sightings in the area.

This is a preliminary information report from the FAA's Washington Operations Center — essentially a first-pass incident log, not a full investigation. It records a sighting reported by the crew of Virgin Atlantic flight 156, a Boeing 787-9 travelling from Las Vegas to London Heathrow on 10 March 2023. At around 03:00 Central Standard Time, while flying east-northeast at flight level 370 (37,000 feet) near Fort Dodge, Iowa, the crew reported seeing three lights at their 11 o'clock position. The lights had varying brightness and intensity. Minneapolis Air Route Traffic Control Center (ARTCC) was advised of the sighting. The crew took no evasive action, and no law enforcement was notified. Notably, the report states that multiple other aircraft reported similar sightings in the same area, though the identities and details of those aircraft are not included in the document. The FAA report does not offer any conclusion about what the lights were. It contains no radar data, sensor readings, or photographic evidence — it is based entirely on pilot testimony relayed through air traffic control. No follow-up investigation is referenced in the document.

What to know

  • This is a preliminary FAA operations report, not a completed investigation or official conclusion about what the lights were.
  • The report is based solely on pilot testimony relayed through air traffic control — no radar tracks, sensor data, or photographs are referenced.
  • While the report mentions multiple aircraft saw similar things, no details about those other aircraft or their observations are provided.
  • The document has low evidence of redaction, meaning what you see is likely close to the full content — but that content is itself limited in scope.
  • A preliminary report like this is a first record of an event, not an assessment of its cause or significance.
