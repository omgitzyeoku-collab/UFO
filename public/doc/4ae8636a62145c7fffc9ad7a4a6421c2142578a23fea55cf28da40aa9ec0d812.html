United Airlines crew reported small red-blue object near Denver

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

An FAA incident report documents that the crew of United Airlines Flight 1835, an Airbus A319 flying from Denver to Knoxville, reported seeing a small red and blue unidentified object below their aircraft at 11,000 feet on June 15, 2024. The object was not picked up on air traffic control radar, and no evasive action was taken.

This document is an incident report from the FAA's Washington Operations Center, relayed through Denver TRACON (Terminal Radar Approach Control, the facility that manages air traffic around Denver). It records a sighting reported by the crew of United Airlines Flight 1835, an Airbus A319 operating from Denver (DEN) to Knoxville (TYS) on June 15, 2024. While flying southbound at 11,000 feet, approximately 10 miles south of Denver, the crew reported seeing a 4-foot red and blue unidentified anomalous phenomenon (UAP) below their aircraft. The crew did not take evasive action. Air traffic control checked their radar and did not detect the object. The report contains a typo in the original text ('OBERSVED' instead of 'observed'), suggesting this is a raw, preliminary report rather than a reviewed final document. The FAA report does not offer any explanation or identification of the object. It simply records what the crew reported and notes the absence of radar confirmation. No additional investigation or follow-up is documented in this record.

What to know

  • This is a single-source pilot report with no corroborating radar data or independent witnesses.
  • The size estimate of 4 feet is a visual approximation made from a moving aircraft at altitude and may not be accurate.
  • The document appears to be a raw preliminary report, not a reviewed final document, based on typographical errors in the original text.
  • The FAA report does not draw any conclusions about what the object was — it only records the crew's account.
  • The incident location (10 miles south of Denver) carries slightly lower confidence as it is derived from shorthand notation rather than a full description.
