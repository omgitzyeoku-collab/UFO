Pilot Reports Possible Parachute Near Atlanta Airport in 2023

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

This is an FAA Washington Operations Center notification from February 2023 in which a pilot flying a Piaggio P180 aircraft reported seeing a possible parachute about two miles east of Atlanta's Hartsfield-Jackson airport. The pilot took no evasive action, and local law enforcement was notified.

On February 16, 2023, at 10:39 AM Eastern time, the FAA's Washington Operations Center logged an incident notification involving an unidentified aerial object near Atlanta, Georgia. The report was filed under record number 237UAP00473. The sighting came from the pilot of a Piaggio P180 aircraft, registered as N189LW, which was flying a route from ATL (Atlanta) to DTS (Destin, Florida). The pilot reported seeing what appeared to be a possible parachute approximately two miles east of Atlanta's airport — one of the busiest airspaces in the world. The coordinates recorded in the report place the sighting in the Atlanta metro area near Hartsfield-Jackson International Airport. The pilot did not take evasive action in response to the object. Local law enforcement was notified of the sighting. This document is a preliminary incident notification, not a full investigation report. It records what the pilot reported but does not include any follow-up findings, radar data, or corroborating observations from other sources. The FAA did not reach a conclusion about what the object was — the pilot's own description was uncertain, using the word "possible" before "parachute."

What to know

  • This is a single-source pilot report. No corroborating radar data or visual confirmation from other observers is mentioned in the document.
  • The pilot described the object as a 'possible parachute' — the identification was uncertain, not definitive.
  • This is a preliminary notification only, not a completed investigation. There is no indication the FAA reached a final conclusion about the object.
  • Redaction levels are low, meaning most of the document's content appears to be intact and readable.
  • The coordinates place the sighting near one of the world's busiest airports, which means routine aerial objects (parachutes, drones, balloons) are plausible explanations, though none was confirmed.
