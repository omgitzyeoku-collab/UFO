Citizen Reports Fast-Moving Objects Near McAllen, Texas in 2023

FAA 2023 ✓ verified against source

This FAA incident report documents a citizen's sighting of rapidly maneuvering unidentified objects northwest of McAllen, Texas on May 31, 2023. The caller recorded two videos and reported that the objects disappeared when a commercial airplane approached. The FAA closed the case after referring the caller to the UFO reporting center.

This document is an FAA SKYWATCH incident report filed under the code "Unidentified Aerial Phenomenon." It records a call from a citizen who observed rapidly maneuvering objects northwest of McAllen, Texas, beginning at approximately 0300 on May 31, 2023. The report was logged by the FAA's Denver headquarters (HQ DEN) at 05:25 that same morning. The caller said the objects appeared around 3:00 a.m. and recorded two videos starting around 3:30 a.m. According to the report, the objects were maneuvering in the area and disappeared whenever a commercial airplane came near. The FAA's response was limited. Denver headquarters provided the caller with the UFO reporting center's phone number, sourced from the agency's internal reference manual (the S-Book). The report was tagged with a secondary code of "NOTIFICATION-LATE," indicating a delay in filing. The case status is listed as closed, with no resolution, investigation, or explanation documented. The airport code MFE — McAllen Miller International Airport — appears as both the origin and destination, which likely reflects the location of the sighting rather than a specific flight path. No radar data, pilot reports, or other corroborating evidence is referenced in the document.

What to know

  • This is a single-source civilian report with no corroborating radar data, pilot observations, or official investigation noted.
  • The two videos referenced in the report are not included — their content is described secondhand by the caller.
  • The FAA closed the case without any documented resolution or explanation, simply referring the caller to the UFO reporting center.
  • The report was flagged as NOTIFICATION-LATE, meaning there was a delay between the sighting and the filing.
  • No named witnesses, flight crew, or additional observers are identified in the document.
