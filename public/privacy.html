Privacy policy

Plain version: we don't sell your data, we don't track you across the web, we don't run ads. The only data we keep tied to an identifiable person is the email address you give us if you ask for Release-notification emails.

Last updated: 2026-05-25

What we collect

Aggregate analytics

We use Plausible Analytics to count page views and see which sections of the archive are used. Plausible does not use cookies, does not track you across sites, does not store IP addresses, and does not build user profiles. The data they receive is anonymous and aggregated. Plausible's data policy.

Aggregate counters tell us "X people viewed the Roswell entity page today." They do not tell us "user 12345 viewed the Roswell page at 14:32."

Server logs

Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps short-term server logs for abuse prevention and uptime monitoring. These include IP address, user-agent, and the path you requested. Vercel retains them on the order of days and we do not access them for any purpose other than incident response. Vercel's privacy policy.

Email for Release notifications

If you enter your email in the "Release 3 incoming" signup strip, we store it for one purpose: emailing you when a new US government UAP release lands and our site has mirrored it. We do not share that email with anyone. We do not send marketing. You can unsubscribe with a one-click link in every email, and your address is deleted from our list at that point.

GitHub interactions

If you open an issue, send a pull request, or report a correction via GitHub, your interaction is governed by GitHub's own privacy policy. We have no control over GitHub's data practices.

What we do NOT collect

  • No third-party advertising trackers (no Google Ads, no Meta Pixel, no TikTok, no AdSense)
  • No social media tracking pixels
  • No cookies for tracking purposes (Plausible is cookieless)
  • No fingerprinting
  • No location data beyond country-level aggregates from Plausible
  • No personal data shared with brokers, advertisers, or governments unless legally compelled

Your rights

Whether you're in the EU (GDPR), UK (UK GDPR), California (CCPA), or anywhere else, you have the right to:

  • Know what data of yours we hold
  • Ask us to delete any data tied to you
  • Receive a copy of any data tied to you
  • Opt out of any future processing

To exercise any of these rights, email info@uapfiles.org. We will respond within 30 days. The only identifying data we typically hold is an email you gave us for notifications, so most requests are simple "please delete my email" requests and we handle them within hours.

Children

UAP Files is a public archive of declassified government documents. We do not intentionally collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will note it at the top of this page and announce it on the homepage. The last-updated date above is authoritative.

Contact

For privacy questions: info@uapfiles.org. We aim to reply within two working days.
