About UAP Files

UAP Files is a free, public archive that translates US government UAP / UFO disclosure documents into plain English. Every claim is source-cited and verifiable against the original document.

What this is

On 8 May 2026 the US Department of War launched PURSUE (Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters): a public release of declassified UAP documents from across the federal government. The site itself is a static gallery — to actually understand any document, you have to click through, download a PDF, decode government acronyms, parse redactions, and somehow build a coherent picture. For most people that's a non-starter.

We solve that. Each document gets a plain-English headline, a 2-sentence summary, a short narrative explaining what it is and what it says, key verified facts, and explicit caveats about evidentiary weight. Every claim is source-cited so you can verify it for yourself.

What we are not

  • Not a UFO advocacy site. We don't claim documents prove or disprove anything. We translate what they say.
  • Not a debunking site. We don't dismiss claims. Where the documents themselves contain analysis, we report it.
  • Not anonymous. The pipeline, the source code, and every transformation are public. Audit us.
  • Not paywalled. Free for everyone, forever. Donations support compute and hosting.

Who's behind this

Independent, non-academic, non-press, non-commercial. Maintained pseudonymously to keep editorial focus on the documents rather than the editor. Source code, manifest, and verification pipeline are all public on GitHub.

Citing UAP Files

To cite a document, link to its detail URL: https://uapfiles.org/#doc/<sha256>. The sha256 is shown on each detail page and is byte-identical to the file in the GitHub Release.

Contact + corrections

If you spot an error, the "report an error" link on each document creates a GitHub issue. We treat every report seriously — accuracy is the project's only currency.
