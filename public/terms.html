Accuracy

We use a multi-agent pipeline (extractor → verifier → adjudicator → citation-linker → rewriter) to minimise errors. Every public claim is tagged with a confidence tier: green (independently verified against a source quote), amber (partially substantiated, hedged in prose), or red (hidden from the public site because we could not verify).

We try hard, but we make mistakes. If you spot one, please file a correction — every document page has a "report error" link. Verified corrections are logged publicly at /corrections.

Citations of an original document do not constitute endorsement of any claim within that document by us. A US government investigator writing "this object behaved anomalously" is reporting their assessment; us mirroring that statement is reporting that their assessment exists, not endorsing it.
